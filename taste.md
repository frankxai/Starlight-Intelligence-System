# Starlight Intelligence System Taste Authority

This is the repository-local decision layer for SIS product, editorial, and evidence surfaces. It consumes the immutable Starlight design kernel; it does not impose one visual skin across the portfolio.

## Surface modes

### Operations

- Keep the shell stable while state changes inside it.
- Put status, owner, risk, and next action within the operator's scan path.
- Favor dense, calm grouping over cinematic composition.
- Make primary actions clear, reversible, and explicit about their scope.

### Evidence

- Place traces, provenance, score deltas, receipts, and failure detail beside the decision they support.
- Distinguish observed facts, inference, and unresolved state.
- Never use visual confidence as a substitute for cited evidence.
- Preserve the reading surface while validation or background work is running.

### Editorial

- Teach through a real workflow, artifact, or system state.
- Translate technical depth without removing inspectable sources.
- Use celestial language only to explain topology or relationships.
- Keep availability, capability, and provenance claims tied to an owner and an as-of source.

## Visual hierarchy

1. Real product states and evidence stacks.
2. Source-linked system diagrams and trace views.
3. Authored supporting media only when it clarifies a real mechanism.

Generated media is never evidence. Generic sci-fi dashboards, radiant particle fields, decorative glow, unaligned nodes, and mythic Arcanea language do not belong on SIS surfaces.

## Motion discipline

Motion communicates scan, trace, confidence resolution, or escalation. It must not compete with logs or move a dashboard during reading.

- Micro response: 90–140 ms.
- Standard state change: 180–260 ms.
- System transition: 300–420 ms.
- Explanatory trace: 500–800 ms, only when causality benefits.
- Reduced motion: render the final connected state with opacity or immediate status changes.

## Release standard

A public surface is not complete until mobile behavior, reduced motion, claim evidence, and failure states are inspectable. Public action follows the pinned SIS pack route: independent verification and a named human.

design.md remains the local asset handoff. When it conflicts with this authority or the pinned SIS brand pack, evidence legibility and the immutable pack win.
