## 3 · Visual Rules & Forbidden Aesthetics
- **Rules:** JetBrains Mono typography, 1px rules, tabular formatting, clean vector geometries. Keep colors within the Starlight Substrate range (emerald `#10b981`, cyan `#06b6d4`, star-teal `#2a5c5c`, void `#0a0a0b`).
- **Forbidden:** Illustrative fantasy art, colorful cartoon styles, noisy textures, and unaligned nodes.
