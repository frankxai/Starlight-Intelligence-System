## Full Style Breakdown (How to Recreate)
**Theme**: Magical girl anime (Sailor Moon, Puella Magi Madoka Magica) + high-fantasy cosmic protectors. High school students by day, star-powered warriors fighting the Void. "Burn bright then burn out" — luminous, hopeful, slightly tragic.

**Core Visual Elements** (must include for fidelity):
- **Motif**: Four-pointed stars (curved outward; team language). Inverted for "dark" characters. Star emblems on outfits, crowns, familiars.
- **Outfits**: Flowing elegant magical girl uniforms. Exaggerated shapes, luminous layers, puffy ribbons, wispy feathers, floaty star petals. Iridescent/sateen white bodice + gloves. Crystals, star accents. Prestige = silk/gold luxury.
- **Colors**: Bright, pastel-vibrant, personality-driven. Team cohesion but individual distinction. Sparkly, luminous.
  - Leader (Ahri): Pinks, peaches, purples.
  - Dark (Syndra): Deep purples + magenta.
  - Fiery: Reds/oranges.
  - Pure/Healer: Whites, blues, greens.
  - General: Pastels with iridescent whites, gold accents.
- **Effects & Lighting**: Heavy starlight, sparkles, glowing magic particles, ethereal energy. Soft cinematic lighting with strong magical glows, rim lights, highlights. Volumetric feel, bokeh stars.
- **Composition**: Dynamic heroic/action poses. Storytelling (energy blasts, floating, protection, team synergy). Strong silhouettes. Backgrounds: Starry cosmic void + subtle energy or "school" elegance. High detail rendering.
- **Art Style**: Riot splash art — high-resolution 2D digital illustration/painting. Anime influence with polished high-fantasy. Intricate textures, professional concept art quality. Not low-effort cute.

**For Starlight Integration** (your Queen DNA):
- Ethereal strong female figure + flowing robes/hair as light streams → now as starlight/cosmic energy.
- Swarms → glowing star familiars or team Guardians.
- Dark cosmic base (#060609 + violet #a78bfa / cyan #67e8f9) → infused with pastel starlight glows and sparkles.
- Premium 3D quality + chibi for Codex → blend with 2D splash polish or pure illustration.
