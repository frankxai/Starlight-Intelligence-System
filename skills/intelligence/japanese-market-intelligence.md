# Japanese Market Intelligence (日本市場インテリジェンス)

> *"Bridging global frontier AI architectures with the Japanese technology ecosystem, language nuances, and market dynamics."*

## When This Skill Activates

- Inquiries or workflows touching the Japanese AI ecosystem, research labs, or developer platforms.
- Keywords: "Japanese AI", "Zenn", "Qiita", "Keigo", "Sakana AI", "Japanese market", "Japan tech", "Sonkeigo", "Kenjougo"
- Tasks requiring culturally calibrated Japanese business communication or market synthesis.

## What This Skill Does

Provides autonomous scanning, synthesis, and cultural calibration for Japanese AI markets, technology releases, and developer communities. Handles technical extraction from Japanese developer hubs, Keigo-level language calibration, and cross-vault contradiction checks against global strategy.

## Procedures

### Procedure 1: Developer Community & Research Scraping

1. **Zenn / Qiita Extraction**: Monitor and synthesize top technical articles in LLM fine-tuning, agent harnesses, local quantized models, and CUDA/ONNX optimization.
2. **Lab & Research Tracking**: Track preprint releases from leading Japanese institutions (Tokyo University, RIKEN, Sakana AI, Preferred Networks, CyberAgent AI Lab).
3. **Model Benchmark Analysis**: Cross-reference Japanese LLM evaluation benchmarks (Nejumi Leaderboard, JGLUE, Swallow benchmarks) against global frontier models.

### Procedure 2: Business Keigo & Cultural Calibration Engine

1. **Sonkeigo (尊敬語 — Respectful Language)**: Apply for user-facing executive briefings, board packets, and external client communications.
2. **Kenjougo (謙譲語 — Humble Language)**: Apply for agent actions and system operations (e.g., 弊社システムにて処理いたしました / 当方にて精査の上ご報告申し上げます).
3. **Teineigo (丁寧語 — Polite Baseline)**: Standard polite tone (〜です / 〜ます) for interactive assistant responses.
4. **Context Nuance Check**: Ensure business terminology matches current Japanese AI corporate vocabulary (e.g., 生成AI, DX推進, 基盤モデル, エージェント基盤).

### Procedure 3: Market Contradiction & Strategic Synthesis

1. Compare incoming Japanese market trends against the Strategic Vault (`memory/vaults/strategic/`).
2. Identify divergence or adoption acceleration in enterprise LLM pricing, regulatory stances (e.g., METI guidelines, IPA AI safety standards), or hardware constraints.
3. Record confirmed market patterns and strategic opportunities into the Strategic and Wisdom vaults.

## Integration Points

- **Vaults**: Strategic Vault (market shifts, regulatory updates), Wisdom Vault (cross-cultural patterns)
- **Agents**: Orchestrator, Prime, Navigator, Hermes
- **Tools**: Hermes web search, memory recall, document export

## Quality Criteria

- Are technical terms from Japanese AI literature translated with accurate industry equivalents?
- Is Keigo formality calibrated appropriately for the target persona and context?
- Are market insights grounded in verified sources (Zenn, Qiita, arXiv, official press releases)?
