# Mission Graph Design

## Outcome

Produce a Mission Contract that traverses a named capability subgraph and ends in defensible work rather than content completion.

## Use this skill when

- A capability graph must become a real project, studio brief, lab, or apprenticeship task.
- A learner needs the smallest mission that can produce evidence for a target capability set.
- An academy pack needs a complete free reference mission.

Do not use it when:

- The request is merely to outline lessons, lectures, or reading lists.
- The beneficiary, artifact, evaluator, or evidence threshold cannot yet be named.

## Inputs

- Versioned capability graph and target node set
- Learner baseline, constraints, consent, and available tools
- Real or simulated beneficiary and accountable human
- Artifact and evaluation requirements

## Procedure

1. Select the smallest connected target subgraph and state why each node is necessary for the intended outcome.
   - Proof: Target nodes and prerequisite closure are explicit.
2. Define the mission contract: beneficiary, problem, accountable human, success event, timebox, constraints, stop conditions, and free execution path.
   - Proof: A complete contract can be accepted or rejected without hidden context.
3. Specify observable artifacts, checkpoints, provenance capture, permitted assistance, and required human decisions.
   - Proof: Every target capability maps to at least one artifact claim and evidence event.
4. Compose the minimum agent team. Give each role bounded tools, data, memory, autonomy, budget, escalation, and termination; downgrade unjustified agents to skills.
   - Proof: An Agent Team Manifest or an explicit no-agent decision.
5. Attach an evaluator and rubric to each consequential claim, including adversarial tests, accessibility, recovery, and appeal paths.
   - Proof: No exit claim depends on attendance, opaque scoring, or payment status.
6. Run a dry traversal for happy path, failure path, prompt-injection path, privacy breach, and incomplete evidence.
   - Proof: A mission validation report with unresolved blockers.

## Outputs

- `mission-contract.json`
- Target-subgraph map
- Artifact and evidence plan
- Agent Team Manifest or no-agent receipt
- Evaluation, recovery, and appeal plan

## Guardrails

- The complete reference mission must be possible without payment; paid service may change speed, privacy, compute, or accountable support only.
- Agent assistance is disclosed at the artifact and claim level.
- Agents may coach, critique, retrieve, validate, and prepare; they may not award human credentials.
- Do not turn a sponsor problem into uncompensated learner labor or transfer learner IP by default.

## Completion

Return the contract, graph coverage, dry-run findings, permissions needing approval, and the exact learner-visible success event.
