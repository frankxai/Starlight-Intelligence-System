## Research grounding

- **Kevin Kelly — *1,000 True Fans*:** the patron-economy math that turns deep audience into sustainable practice. Direction-cited (specific dollar figures vary by practitioner cost structure).
- **Cory Doctorow on enshittification:** platform-decay arc; relevance for why algorithmic-distribution is structurally untrustworthy as primary channel.
- **Stay-interview research direction (extending HR stay-interview to fan-relationship):** anonymous-with-attribution-trust trumps anonymous-no-attribution; question framing matters for honest signal.
- **Email-marketing and newsletter-economy research direction:** list quality dominates list size; engaged-segment open rates exceed unengaged-broadcast open rates by significant margins (specific figures vary).
- **Subscription-economy research (Tien Tzuo et al. — *Subscribed*):** patron-tier architecture; engagement-vs-acquisition cost economics.

This skill cites direction. Specific list-and-patron figures vary by practitioner; the architecture (cohort segmentation; ritual cadence; list as sovereign layer; stay-interview discipline; sovereign-publish pyramid) is stable.

## Protocol — 7 steps

### Step 1: Sort audience stage

Pre-list (no email list yet) / early-list (<500 subscribers, low engagement) / growing-list (500-5000, building rhythm) / mature-list (5000+, full ritual cadence) / patron-tier-active / multi-tier (list + patrons + peers active).

### Step 2: Vision-boundary read

Pull MEMORY.md vision boundaries. If practitioner's vision refuses certain monetization patterns (e.g., refuses-upsell-aggression; refuses-patron-tiers-as-paywall-for-basic-engagement), audience architecture respects that.

### Step 3: Cohort map

Segment by three axes:

- **Entry-point:** which release / placement / show brought this person in?
- **Depth:** casual (algorithmic listener; never opted in) / engaged (opted into list; opens occasionally) / patron (paid tier) / collaborator (creative or operational relationship).
- **Channel:** list (sovereign) / Bandcamp (semi-sovereign) / Patreon (semi-sovereign) / DSP (algorithmic) / Discord-Geneva-private-community (semi-sovereign).

The map is the basis for differentiated communication. Broadcast-to-everyone-the-same is refused.

### Step 4: Ritual cadence

Across five cadences:

- **Daily:** if the practitioner has daily rhythm, what is it (daily voice memo to patrons; daily lyric snippet to engaged-list). Daily is not mandatory.
- **Weekly:** the load-bearing cadence for most practitioners (Friday list-letter; Sunday studio-update; Tuesday-night listening party).
- **Monthly:** monthly milestone (release-month ritual; first-of-month patron-only drop; monthly long-form essay).
- **Quarterly:** quarterly arc theme (recording quarter; release quarter; touring quarter; recovery-and-writing quarter).
- **Annual:** yearly retrospective; year-in-listening; end-of-year thank-you to patrons; annual show or annual residency.

Each ritual: cohort served + belonging mechanism + success indicator.

### Step 5: List architecture

The list as sovereign distribution layer.

- **Segmentation:** at minimum: engaged (recent open) / dormant (no opens 90+ days) / new (signed up <30 days; in welcome sequence) / patron (paying tier overlap). More segments where catalog has natural divisions.
- **Welcome sequence:** 5-7 messages over 21 days. Introduces practitioner's voice, catalog arc, cadence rhythm, patron tier (with consent — no immediate hard pitch).
- **Cadence:** weekly is the most common load-bearing cadence; monthly is sustainable but reduces compounding; daily is heavy unless voice is built for it.
- **Voice rules:** every list message runs through Genius voice samples; never the platform's default cadence.
- **Opt-in pathways:** clear, single-purpose opt-ins (download-the-instrumental for list opt-in; pre-release-access for list members).
- **Monetization integration:** list as path to Bandcamp / Patreon / direct-product purchases — never the only purpose, never absent.

### Step 6: Fan stay-interview

The most-engaged fans asked what keeps them.

- **Methodology:** stay-interview (extending HR research to fan relationships) — patrons / Bandcamp-loyal / list-engaged asked what keeps them, what would make them leave, what they wish more of, what they wish less of.
- **Anonymous-with-attribution-trust:** name-attributed responses with practitioner's confidentiality promise produce honest signal; anonymous-no-attribution is noisier.
- **Question framing:** "what's the one thing that, if it changed, would make you cancel?" produces better signal than "are you happy?"
- **Pattern recognition:** ≥3-occurrence threshold for theme elevation. Practitioner sees patterns; does not chase slavishly.

### Step 7: Sovereign-publish pyramid

Publishing-as-architecture, not content-calendar.

| Layer | What gets published here |
|---|---|
| **Peers-only** (smallest, deepest) | Technical session; production-detail walk-through; collaboration invitation |
| **Patrons-only** | Alternate-take; unfinished demo; pre-mixed stem-pack; residency multi-track access |
| **List-only** | Unreleased song; studio-process video; long-form essay on catalog arc; first-listen of next release |
| **Public-canonical** (largest reach, smallest depth) | DSP release; YouTube performance video; long-form essay on personal site |

Pyramid is layered, not duplicated. Each layer has its rhythm.

## Rules

1. **Disclaimer at top of every artifact touching monetization.**
2. **Sort audience stage before applying commands.**
3. **Vision-boundary read before architecture decisions.** Practitioner's stance on monetization gates the design.
4. **Cohort map across entry-point + depth + channel.** Broadcast-to-everyone-the-same refused.
5. **Ritual cadence audit across daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / annual.**
6. **List architecture: segmentation + welcome + cadence + voice + opt-in + monetization integration.**
7. **Genius voice composition for fan-facing artifacts. Non-optional.**
8. **Stay-interview cadence quarterly minimum.** Anonymous-with-attribution-trust framing.
9. **Theme elevation discipline: ≥3-occurrence threshold.**
10. **Sovereign-publish pyramid layered, not duplicated.**
11. **Algorithmic-followers-as-audience refused.** Followers without list capture are downstream metrics, not relationships.
12. **Bot followers, fake plays, payola playlists refused.**
13. **Fans-as-marketing-targets framing refused.** The relationship is the asset.
14. **Every artifact ends with "Built on SIP" attestation.**
