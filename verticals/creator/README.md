## What Creator IS does

Creator IS is where excavated frameworks become content systems and products that compound. Composition layer — does not own primary capability; orchestrates across Self IS (what frameworks) and Brand IS (what voice). Feeds Business IS (offers + products land in revenue model).

The IS owns:
- **Multi-modal pipelines** — content calendar across text / audio / video / image, sequenced by leverage and voice.
- **Content systematization** — converting recurring frameworks into reusable production templates.
- **Executor playbook** — handover SOPs for the DELEGATE bucket of the Freedom Path (composes with Self IS).
- **Catalog architecture** — for creator domains where catalog compounds (music, courses, books, art).
- **Voice fidelity discipline** — every artifact runs through Genius voice samples; no generic creator-tech tone leaks.

## Primary commands

- **`/creator-pipeline`** — multi-modal content pipeline from a person's Genius Profile.
- **`/content-systemize`** — convert content pillar into reusable production templates.
- **`/train-executor`** — executor onboarding playbook from Freedom Path DELEGATE bucket.

Vault namespace: `creator/pipeline-<slug>.md` · `creator/templates-<slug>/` · `creator/executor-playbook-<slug>.md`.

## Refusal patterns

- Engagement-bait dressed as art. Trend-chase without craft.
- AI-generated content with no human voice composition (generic tone leakage).
- Catalog architecture that prioritizes algorithmic-feed metrics over compounding value.
- Productization that fragments the genius into unrelated SKUs (offer sprawl).
- Pricing that contradicts the Brand Kit's positioning.
