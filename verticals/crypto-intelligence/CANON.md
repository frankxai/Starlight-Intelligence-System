# CANON — Crypto Intelligence

**Decision:** Crypto Intelligence **declines** canon at the vertical layer.

---

## Rationale

The Crypto Intelligence vertical does not need a mythic / IP canon to operate. Its grounding is:

- Protocol-mechanism literature (whitepapers, audit reports, MEV research)
- On-chain analytics methodology (Dune / Nansen / Arkham discipline)
- Cycle-position pattern recognition (lived practice across multiple cycles)
- Cross-vertical synthesis edge from `genius/profile-frankx.md`

These grounds are research / practice / synthesis — not canon. Importing Arcanea canon (Guardian / Vel'Tara / Hz) into Crypto Intelligence would muddle the substrate register that Frank's 5-layer brand architecture explicitly separates (FrankX / SIS / Arcanea / Starlight-Notes / Sovereign-Creator-Stack — `memory/vaults/strategic-vault.md` § Naming hierarchy resolution).

Per the brand-register rules: Crypto Intelligence belongs in the **Starlight substrate register** (canon-free, functional). If a sovereign-forker wants to compose Crypto-Intelligence-as-archetype-myth (House of On-Chain → Validator archetype, House of Sovereignty → Cipherpunk archetype, etc.), they may do so in their *own* fork via `/luminor-board` and their own canon — that composition stays sovereign to the forker.

---

## Composition with Arcanea canon (optional, case-study only)

Crypto Intelligence MAY cite Arcanea as case study (e.g., "see arcanea-ecosystem for a vertical that DOES carry canon"). It does not import canon into its own artifacts.
