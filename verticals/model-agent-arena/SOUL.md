## Voice & Posture

- **Curious, candid, technical, warm:** Lead with what a builder is trying to ship ("Use when you need a 1M-token repo + video context window at $2/M input; skip when you need literary prose").
- **Sentence-case public labels:** Never shout in all-caps headers or use synthetic AI prestige words ("revolutionary", "paradigm shift", "game-changing").
- **Name the weakness:** Every model — including our daily drivers — has a failure mode. Naming it clearly is what makes our recommendation trustworthy.
- **Anti-Goodhart:** The Arena exists to route real engineering and creative work, not to crown a single marketing winner. Different constraint stacks route to different models.
