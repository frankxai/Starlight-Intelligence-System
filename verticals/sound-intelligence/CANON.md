# CANON — Sound Intelligence

> Canon declaration for this vertical. Per SIP, every vertical declares its canon posture explicitly: defines / extends / adopts / declines. Silent canon use is a breach.

---

## Posture

**This vertical declines defining its own canon. It optionally composes with Arcanea Hz canon for frequency-grounded work where the practitioner adopts that grounding.**

Sound Intelligence is theory- and cognition-grounded, not canon-grounded. Its substrate is the published literature on music perception, composition cognition, mix architecture, mastering theory, and sync economics — not a proprietary fictional or aesthetic universe. Defining a canon would invent a layer the practice does not need and would corrode the synthesis the vertical is built on.

The vertical operates on **theory-and-research direction** (Levitin on music perception, Huron on expectation-and-reward, Patel on music-language cognition, Margulis on repetition, Moylan on mix architecture, Katz on mastering, Bregman on auditory scene analysis, Pohjannoro on composition cognition, Bertin-Mahieux et al. on hit prediction, Pachet on style modeling, et al.) — cited by direction, not invented. Theory is not canon; it is the public scientific record. The vertical does not claim it, license it, or wrap it.

## Optional composition with Arcanea Hz canon

Where a practitioner adopting this vertical operates on frequency-grounded work — ritual music tuned to specific Hz, vibe-architecture composition using the Arcanea Hz mapping, or a deliberately Hz-tuned catalog — they may compose with Arcanea Hz canon under its CC-BY-NC license.

**Composition rules if adopted:**

- License terms accepted explicitly. Attribution to Arcanea Hz canon ships on every artifact that uses Hz mappings.
- Composition is per-track or per-release, not vertical-wide. A practitioner does not adopt-or-decline Arcanea canon at the vertical level; they adopt it at the artifact level where it composes naturally (e.g., a frequency-grounded album adopts; a non-frequency-grounded single does not).
- Decline is the default. Most Sound Intelligence work has no Arcanea canon dependency.
- The Hz mapping is a creative grounding choice, not a science claim. The vertical does not claim "440Hz is wrong, 432Hz heals" or any of the related folkloric assertions; it offers Hz grounding as an aesthetic and ritual structuring tool the practitioner may adopt with eyes open.

## What declining canon means in practice

- The vertical does not introduce proprietary frameworks-as-canon (e.g., a "Twelve Pillars of Sound Practice" structure that other practitioners must adopt to compose).
- The vertical does not gate composition behind canonical adoption — any practitioner can fork and operate without inheriting any canonical content.
- The vertical does not claim authority over any particular musical genre, theory school, or mix philosophy. The practitioner's specific synthesis (their chord language, their mix signature, their lyric voice) is their IP; the vertical's substrate is the file contract and refusal posture, not the content.
- Cross-vertical composition (e.g., Sound Intelligence + HR Intelligence + Genius IS in one practitioner's full stack — for an artist who also runs a label and hires producers) does not require any canon negotiation — there is no canon to negotiate.

---

## What this leaves room for

A practitioner forking this vertical may, in their own private fork, **define their own canon for their practice** — their proprietary frameworks, their voice fingerprint, their named methodologies (e.g., a producer's named mix-philosophy methodology, a composer's named harmonic system, a label's named A&R framework). That canon is the practitioner's IP, not the substrate's. The practitioner declares it in their own fork's `CANON.md`, replacing this declined-canon declaration with their own defines-canon declaration.

The reference scaffold declines, so the substrate stays content-neutral. Practitioners build their own.

---

## Music-specific canon hygiene

A note on a domain-specific canon trap: the music industry is rich with folkloric canon (Mozart effect, 432Hz healing, "all the great records were tracked to tape," "modern music is too loud / too compressed / too sample-driven"). The vertical refuses to import any of these as canon-grade claims. They are folklore. The practitioner may use them aesthetically (a track tuned to 432Hz is a creative choice, not a healing claim) but the vertical does not ground recommendations on them.

The boundary: theory and research, cited by direction. Folklore is named as folklore, not laundered into recommendation language.
