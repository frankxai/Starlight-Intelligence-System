# LABELS — Arcanea Records (four-label canonical map)

> Master map of Arcanea Records' four sub-labels. Each label has a canonical sound DNA, visual DNA, voice DNA, audience contract, and monetization stack. Personas sit under labels; personas inherit constraint from label-canon and contribute differentiation.

---

## Label discipline

The four-label structure is the moat. Every label is sharp; every label has a different audience contract; every label has a different monetization stack. Genre-blur tracks ship as Arcanea-canon experiments or stay in `catalog/draft/`. Releases that "could fit Frank Riemer or Frank's Vibes" probably belong to neither.

Each label's full canon lives at `verticals/music-is/labels/<slug>/CANON.md`. This file is the index + at-a-glance map.

## 1. Frank Riemer — neo-classical / cinematic-piano

**Slug:** `frank-riemer`
**Position:** the artist-of-record label. Frank himself is the artist.
**Sound DNA:**
- Neo-classical foundation: piano-led, often solo piano or piano + strings
- Cinematic-grade arrangement: dynamic range protected (mastered for dynamic-range delivery, not streaming-loudness norms)
- Contemplative, slow-build, motif-driven (Olafur Arnalds × Max Richter × Nils Frahm reference triangle)
- Harmonic vocabulary: modal mixture, sus chords, occasional polytonality; resolves mostly tonal
- Tempo: 60-90 BPM typical; rubato common
- No vocals (or rare ambient vocal pad with disclosure)

**Visual DNA:**
- Monochrome or low-saturation palette; cool-grey, near-black, single warm accent
- Negative space; minimalist; texture-of-paper, texture-of-piano-felt
- No persona face on covers (label is artist-of-record, not persona-multiplied)

**Voice DNA (artist letters, sync pitches, social):**
- First person, contemplative, technical-when-needed
- Direct: "this piece took 14 attempts at the bridge"
- Refuses: marketing-speak, hype, "vibes" descriptions

**Audience contract:**
- Deep-work listeners (focus playlists)
- Long-form-listening (full-album)
- Film/TV music supervisors (sync candidates)
- Score-grade contemplative listeners

**Monetization stack:**
- **Streaming:** steady-but-not-headline — playlist placement on Deep Focus, Peaceful Piano, Calm, Stress Relief
- **Sync:** highest leverage — film, TV, prestige docu, ad (premium brands)
- **Bandcamp:** direct + name-your-price + occasional limited (Phase 1+)
- **Limited edition vinyl** (Phase 6) — one pressing per year max
- **Sheet music + stem packs** (Phase 5+) — for piano students, score readers

**Release cadence:** 1 release per quarter target (single-grade); 1 album per year target (Phase 2+).

## 2. Frank's Vibes — electronic / lo-fi / chill-house / vibe-engineered

**Slug:** `franks-vibes`
**Position:** vibe-engineered electronic label. Designed for context-of-listening — gym, journaling, evening, creator-soundtrack, lo-fi-study.
**Sound DNA:**
- Electronic foundation: lo-fi beats, chill-house, vibe-electronic, downtempo
- Production: warm low-end, moderate compression, deliberately lower dynamic range than Frank Riemer (streaming-optimized)
- Vocals: occasional, often chopped/processed; rare clear lead vocal
- Tempo bands: 70-95 (chill/lo-fi/journal), 95-115 (gym/movement), 115-128 (evening-vibe)
- Sub-genres operate as vibe-cohorts not as standalone identities

**Visual DNA:**
- Sunset-toned palette; warm gradients; aurora-like; mid-saturation
- Persona-led: each persona has a face/avatar (real or stylized)
- Mood-context-explicit (gym shot, sunset window, headphones-on-train)

**Voice DNA:**
- Per persona; second-person common ("for your evening reset")
- Vibe-context-aware copy
- Refuses: producer-influencer YouTube voice, gear-flex, "exclusive drop" hype

**Audience contract:**
- Creator-soundtrack listeners
- Gym crowd (specific-tempo sub-cohort)
- Journaling crowd
- Evening-vibe playlists
- Algorithmic discovery via tempo + mood matching

**Monetization stack:**
- **Streaming:** primary — volume play, playlist placement is the lever
- **Spotify Canvas** drives discovery; per-release Canvas non-optional
- **Sync (lifestyle/brand/ad):** mid-leverage — direct-to-brand pitches via persona
- **TikTok-as-discovery:** Phase 3+ via Claw-TT; never as primary distribution
- **Fan-tier (Phase 6):** ConvertKit list when warranted

**Release cadence:** 2-4 releases/month per active persona; multi-persona scaling Phase 4+.

## 3. Arcanea — Guardian / cinematic / mythic / score-grade

**Slug:** `arcanea`
**Position:** canon-bound mythic label. Composes with Arcanea ecosystem canon. Each persona maps to one Guardian or canonical worldbuilding archetype.
**Sound DNA:**
- Cinematic foundation: orchestral hybrid (live strings + synths), score-grade, mythic-scale
- Reference triangle: Hans Zimmer (epic) × Two Steps from Hell (trailer) × Ramin Djawadi (game/TV score)
- Mythic, grandiose, narrative-driven; tension-and-release across long forms
- Harmonic: modal, often Lydian / Phrygian for mythic moments; epic minor for tension
- Tempo: 70-160 BPM range depending on Guardian-archetype mapping
- Vocals: choral, languageless, mythic-cadence (no English lyrics typically)

**Visual DNA:**
- Per-Guardian palette (12 Guardians × 12 palette systems per Arcanea canon)
- Cinematic concept-art aesthetic; hyper-realistic + mythic
- Persona maps 1:1 to Guardian; persona visual is Guardian visual

**Voice DNA:**
- Mythic / oracular cadence; per-Guardian voice
- Composes with Arcanea ecosystem canon (existing Guardian canon documents authoritative)
- Refuses: fantasy-genre clichés, generic "epic" copy, score-music-influencer voice

**Audience contract:**
- Arcanea ecosystem fans (cross-amplification with the broader brand)
- Score-grade listeners (film, TV, game)
- Mythic / fantasy / canon-fans
- Worldbuilding community

**Monetization stack:**
- **Streaming:** compounds via canon-loyalty; cross-pollinated with Arcanea audience
- **Sync (film/TV/game):** highest leverage; Guardian-archetype matching for placement briefs
- **NFT / limited edition** (Phase 6) — Guardian-canon-tied minted via Sound.xyz / Catalog
- **Fan-tier (Phase 4+):** Arcanea Discord; cross-promo with broader Arcanea ecosystem
- **Composes with Voice & Video IS** for narrative music videos (Phase 4+)

**Release cadence:** 1 release per Guardian per year minimum (Phase 2+); 1 album per year per active Guardian-persona (Phase 3+).

**Canon binding:** every Arcanea-label release names the Guardian-archetype in metadata. Releases that don't bind to canon ship as Arcanea-experiments (separate sub-folder) or stay in draft.

## 4. Nona — punk / alt / abrasive / cathartic

**Slug:** `nona`
**Position:** cathartic punk/alt label. Raw, abrasive, peak-state. The label that says "no" to vibe-engineering.
**Sound DNA:**
- Punk / alt foundation: distorted guitars, aggressive drums, raw vocal energy
- Reference triangle: IDLES × Fontaines D.C. × early Bauhaus
- Cathartic, peak-state, rebellion-context
- Harmonic: power chords, modal-based, occasional dissonance for tension
- Tempo: 130-180 BPM typical
- Vocals: raw, cathartic, often shouted/screamed (with consent disclosure if AI-vocal-cloned)

**Visual DNA:**
- High-contrast, monochrome with single rage-color (red, yellow-warning)
- Brutalist typography; xerox aesthetic; collage-grade
- Persona-led but anti-glamour

**Voice DNA:**
- Per persona; raw, direct, refuses-corporate-speak
- First-person, defiant, peak-state
- Refuses: marketing-speak, hype, "vibes," any corporate-aligned aesthetic

**Audience contract:**
- Gym peak-state (specific high-BPM sub-cohort)
- Rebellion-context
- Energy-listeners
- Live-performance candidate (Phase 5+)

**Monetization stack:**
- **Streaming:** volume in fitness/peak-state playlists
- **Merch (Phase 4+):** core revenue lever — t-shirts, posters; brutalist aesthetic
- **Live performance (Phase 5+):** persona-as-live-act-with-AI-disclosure
- **Sync:** sports / fitness / rebellion-context ads; lower-leverage than Frank Riemer or Arcanea

**Release cadence:** 1-2 releases/month per active persona; album-format Phase 3+.

## Cross-label rules

1. **No cross-label release.** A song belongs to one label.
2. **No cross-label persona.** A persona belongs to one label.
3. **Genre-blur tracks** ship as Arcanea-experiments (sub-folder, not main release feed) or stay in draft.
4. **Cross-promotion across labels** allowed via Frank-as-architect channels (frankx.ai, Frank's personal social) with attribution-cascade graph encoded.
5. **Per-label financial split** to Arcanea Records BV; per-persona split to Frank as creator (and any other contributors named per release).
