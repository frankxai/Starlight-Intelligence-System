# Suno Genre Style Cards

> Per-genre prompt anchors and known-working style stems. Composes with `prompt-pattern-library.md` (3-layer pattern) and per-label CANON sound DNA.

**Engine target:** Suno v5
**Last updated:** 2026-04-29 (v0.1 seed)

---

## Style card format

Each card has:
- **Style stem** (the 5-15 word anchor)
- **Tempo band**
- **Vocal posture default**
- **Production posture default**
- **Reference triangle**
- **Banned anchors** (what to avoid in prompt)
- **Pairs with label** (which Music IS labels this card serves)

---

## Card 1 — Neo-classical solo piano

```
Style stem: "neo-classical solo piano, contemplative, dynamic-range-protected"
Tempo band: 60-90 BPM
Vocal: none (or rare ambient pad)
Production: dynamic-range protected, real-room reverb, warm low-end
Reference: Olafur Arnalds × Max Richter × Nils Frahm
Banned anchors: "viral", "tiktok-friendly", "drop", "epic"
Pairs with: Frank Riemer
```
