## Cross-pattern banned (refused for any new persona)

- **Names of identifiable existing artists** (any genre, any era, any country) — refuses copyright-flirting and search-conflict
- **Slurs, ableist terms, terms with active reclamation contention**
- **Generic genre roles alone** — "DJ", "MC", "Producer", "Singer", "Composer", "Rapper" (with no other word)
- **Numerical-only names** — "808", "120", "60Hz" (forgettable, unsearchable)
- **Names that mimic non-music brand trademarks** — "Spotify-X", "Apple Inc Music", "Netflix Productions"
- **Misappropriated cultural names** — deity names, sacred terms, ethnic-specific terms from cultures the artist is not part of (refuse and surface concern)
- **Three-or-more-word names** — forgettability cliff (with rare exceptions for established phrasing patterns)

## Availability-required banned (manual check)

The following classes of names require a manual availability check BEFORE use; they are not blanket-banned but high-risk:

- Common single-syllable nouns (e.g., "Echo", "Pulse", "Drift") — high probability of existing-artist conflict
- Single-name first-name choices (e.g., "Maya", "Sara") — high probability of existing-artist conflict
- Common two-word combos (e.g., "Iron Heart", "Black Sun") — high probability of existing band conflict
- Compounds with "core" suffix (e.g., "Hearcore", "Ravecore") — saturated
- Phonetic alternates of common words (e.g., "Wuv" for "Love", "Hart" for "Heart") — feel manufactured

Run `naming-intelligence` skill availability-check protocol before locking any name in these classes.
