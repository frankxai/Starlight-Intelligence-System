## A defensible pass

1. Normalize case, spacing, punctuation, diacritics, singular/plural, phonetic and spelling variants. Preserve Akamoro/Akamoto and Nonaca/Nona as unresolved distinct strings until founder reconciliation.
2. Search actual music services: Spotify, Apple, YouTube, Beatport/Traxsource where relevant, Bandcamp, SoundCloud and distributor mapping. Save artist IDs, active releases and a dated result/export. Confirm whether a matching catalog is yours.
3. Verify desired social handles and domain checkout/registration records directly. A web page or empty search is not live handle availability. Record ownership separately from availability; acquire nothing from this setup.
4. Search KVK/trade names and BOIP/Benelux, EUIPO/TMview; add other jurisdictions for actual planned markets. Record register query, goods/services/classes, mark IDs, status, owners, filing dates and similar marks. Search source being official does not mean a clearance opinion.
5. Compare actual related goods/services, geography and likely confusion with a qualified reviewer. Counsel determines filing strategy, name-use conditions and whether coexistence is defensible.
6. Record founder choice, written scoped legal review, filing receipt and eventual registration/opposition status as distinct events. Recheck immediately before launch and at significant expansion.

Useful music discussion often includes Nice Classes 9 and 41; clothing or a software product changes scope. Do not mechanically buy every class or promise that these two protect all uses.

## Strategy assessment

Judge fan recall after one hearing, spoken pronunciation, emotional fit, distinction from nearby acts, usefulness on artwork/credits, relationship to the existing brand and the cost of maintaining the act. Record observations or a strategic judgment explicitly. Do not invent a perfect numerical naming score or claim listeners were surveyed.
