Frank X: chosen stage-name direction, exact spacing recorded. Frank Riemer remains the human author/producer and legal identity. FrankX is the existing founder/business styling. Use one consistent approved DSP spelling and real platform artist IDs; punctuation is not a unique-identity guarantee.

## Frank X versus Frank Riemer

The distinction matters where a listener wants an acoustic/piano relationship, where a supervisor needs a cue catalog, and where release imagery, sequencing and expectations differ. Classical genre itself does not require another name. If there are only occasional quiet pieces, publish them as Frank X Sessions. If piano is a durable body of work, a Frank Riemer catalog provides a clear destination without splitting the flagship electronic campaign.

Suno piano may enter either contract when it musically fits, with accurate credits. Do not present generated playing as a recorded Frank performance. Distinguish an original work inspired by classical craft from an actual Bruch/Mozart composition, an arrangement, and a sampled modern recording. Review the exact work, edition and source recording; a historical composer reference is not a clearance shortcut.

## Routing decision

1. Record composition, recording, version, contributor and source IDs before assigning a campaign.
2. Identify who speaks or performs in the recording: Frank, a real collaborator, a fictional character, a collective, or instrumental production.
3. Preserve an existing album contract. *ARCANEA: You Make Me Wonder* retains romantic wonder, intimate voice, the five-note motif and house/liquid-DnB/alt-pop spine. *Starlight in My Mind* retains the female stage narrator and guitar response. Album titles do not automatically name artists.
4. Test the track against a known voice, emotional job and listening occasion. A female generated lead belongs on a human producer record only with truthful credits, not the claim that Frank sang it.
5. Route club DnB/tech house and electronic-rock crossover to Frank X when a coherent era holds them together. Pure piano/chamber work enters the piano proof. World scores enter the ensemble/project proof. Distinct character songs stay in private incubation.
6. Use series, language and campaign tags for variation. Create an artist only when it earns a distinct performer relationship.

The personal *Music for Becoming* program stays adjacent to the two album worlds. Preserve its activity/perspective contracts and winner prompts; do not automatically turn all experiments into public albums.

## Admission test for a new artist

Require a one-sentence listener promise, three coherent finished audition candidates, repeatable voice/visual references, a rights-ready first packet, name clearance, a human owner and funded attention for a 90-day campaign. Compare the act with releasing those same tracks under an existing artist. A private listener bracket should test hook recall, replay desire and identity recognition; no fan survey or audio selection was completed here.

Start with the flagship and at most one genuinely separate public lane. Ocean Muses and Alera can both develop privately; launch only the stronger ready proof first. Increase active roster capacity only after the next six weeks are prepared, existing campaigns are on time and listener response/costs support it.

These are recommended admission rules, not founder-approved release dates or industry success benchmarks.
