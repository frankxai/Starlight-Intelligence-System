# “Can I Be Honest” — Cinematic Rebuild Treatment

Status: approved for production restart

## Why the first video failed

The previous teaser is rejected. It met codec and duration requirements but
failed the creative brief:

- One background behavior carried all 30 seconds.
- No human-scale subject, cinematic plate, performance, location, or evolving
  visual evidence existed.
- Four serif title cards behaved like a template, not a music video.
- The cut did not develop with the song; it merely replaced phrases on a timer.
- Remotion was misused as the source of the visual idea instead of the finishing
  and editorial system.
- The “doorway” metaphor was adequate for a cover/Canvas, not for an entire
  narrative teaser.

Premium score: 15/30 — restart.  
Motion rubric: 27/55 — useful prototype, visibly generic.

## Outcome contract

- Audience: a listener encountering a new intimate artist through a 30-second
  vertical chorus edit.
- Surface: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Spotify Clip candidate.
- First read: two people want to cross an emotional distance but hesitate.
- Evidence: song-specific lyrical actions, tactile physical spaces, coherent
  photographic plates, and an edit that resolves with the chorus.
- Failure modes: generated-romance cliché, perfume-ad glamour, warped hands,
  random montage, constant text, neon, fantasy portal, and slow zooms with no
  editorial consequence.
- Acceptance: Tier B media, 26/30+, 45/55 motion rubric, four-shot contact sheet,
  first-frame/poster proof, compression check, and a second critic pass.

## Motion thesis

The video should feel like breath held before an admission: stillness, tiny
physical signals, then a decisive move across the light. Motion is intimate,
observational, tactile, and unresolved until the final phrase.

Personality: restrained, magnetic, nocturnal, human, analog.

## Visual system

- 9:16 cinematic photography, 24 fps editorial feel delivered at 30 fps.
- Near-black, umber, tobacco gold, warm cream, low saturation.
- Real materials: linen, condensation, sheer curtain, matte plaster, skin,
  window light.
- Camera language: macro push, rack-focus simulation, lateral drift, slow
  handheld imperfection, one final forward move.
- Typography appears only twice: opening question and final answer. It is never
  the primary footage.
- Cut on lyrical intention, not every word.
