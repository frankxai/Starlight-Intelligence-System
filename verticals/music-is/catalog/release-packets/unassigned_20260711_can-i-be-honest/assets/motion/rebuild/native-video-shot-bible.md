# Native Video Shot Bible — The Unsent Room

Status: production-ready structure; casting/artist and native-video access remain
hard gates.

## Locked behavior

- Format: native 9:16, 24 or 30fps source, 1080p minimum.
- Camera: intimate 28/35/50mm equivalents; close physical distance; imperfect
  handheld only when the protagonist moves.
- Palette: tobacco amber, bruised cyan shadows, warm skin, silver glass.
- Texture: humid air, sweat, condensation, perfume haze, practical tungsten.
- Performance: fear disguised as composure; no fashion-model vacancy.
- Continuity: same face, hair, charcoal silk shirt, silver ring, room geography,
  glass, doorway, and second character across every accepted clip.
- Avoid: glossy AI-commercial skin, slow-motion fabric porn, generic nightclub
  montage, random camera orbit, morphing hands, empty beauty shots, lip-sync
  close-ups without verified lyrics.

## Generation protocol

1. Cast and approve the protagonist still set before any motion generation.
2. Generate shots 02, 12, 16, and 17 first. If the artist cannot carry the
   camera, stop; spectacle cannot rescue the campaign.
3. Establish room geography with shots 03 and 06.
4. Generate physical anomalies as isolated inserts, then composite or match-cut.
5. Use accepted end frames as references for the next narrative shot.
6. Generate 3–5 candidates per hero shot, but only one variable per iteration:
   performance, camera, or physics—not all three.
7. Reject identity drift, dead eyes, weightless movement, unstable hands,
   impossible room geography, or camera motion without motivation.
8. Preserve model, prompt, references, duration, seed/job ID, cost, and verdict.

## Acceptance before final edit

- Artist is recognizable in every performance shot.
- At least four shots contain convincing native human movement.
- At least three physical events are surprising but causally readable.
- First 1.5 seconds works without text.
- The question and answer both land on human performance, not only typography.
- No clip survives merely because it was expensive to generate.
- Top two hook openings are identified before the 26-second master is polished.
