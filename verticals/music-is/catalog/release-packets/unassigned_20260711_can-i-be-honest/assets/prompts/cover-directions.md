# Cover Direction — Provisional Concept

Asset tier: B generated-media candidate.  
Final surface: 3000×3000 RGB cover, then 9:16 and 16:9 crops.  
Status: concept only until artist/persona visual DNA is locked.

## Keep

- One song-specific metaphor: the risk of opening a private truth.
- Editorial restraint, tactile analog material, 65–75% negative space.
- Near-black, smoked umber, warm cream, and muted antique gold.
- A physically believable slit of warm light between two dark planes.
- No face, celebrity likeness, logo, title, artist name, watermark, or fake text.

## Avoid

- Generic romance couple, lips, roses, hearts, bedroom glamour.
- Fantasy portal, sci-fi corridor, neon, glossy 3D, bokeh fog.
- Piano imagery inherited from the incompatible Frank Riemer canon.
- Any typography baked into the generated image; exact type is added later.

## Generation prompt

Minimal square album-cover photograph, an intimate dark interior reduced to two
charcoal architectural planes almost touching, a narrow vertical seam of warm
amber light opening between them, subtle suggestion of two human hands near the
edge of the light but not touching and no bodies or faces, tactile linen and
matte plaster textures, restrained analog 35mm grain, deep blacks, smoked umber,
warm cream highlights, emotionally vulnerable and nocturnal, 70 percent
negative space, precise editorial composition, believable physical light,
premium contemporary independent music artwork, no text, no logo, no watermark,
no neon, no fantasy portal, no glossy CGI, no flowers, no hearts.
