# Hook Lab — “Can I Be Honest?”

Status: preproduction; do not publish until artist, lyrics, rights, and release
date are locked.

World: **The Unsent Room** — a nocturnal afterparty freezes and reverses while
the protagonist decides whether to confess.

## Six opening experiments

| ID | First 0.7 seconds | 1–3 seconds | Length | Hypothesis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 Reverse glass | A falling glass snaps upward into the artist’s hand | Direct eye contact: “Can I be honest?” | 9s | Impossible physics earns the stop; face earns continuation |
| H2 Frozen exit | A lover is frozen halfway through a glowing doorway | Artist walks through a crowd suspended mid-dance | 12s | Relationship jeopardy reads before explanation |
| H3 Confession close-up | Extreme close-up; visible inhale; immediate question | Room cuts to black for 12 frames, then reappears frozen | 9s | Human vulnerability plus rupture beats abstract spectacle |
| H4 Perfume rewind | Vapor folds backward into a bottle | Match cut from vapor to the artist’s held breath | 10s | Tactile beauty can stop without losing narrative specificity |
| H5 Living stair | Each footstep lights one warm stair in a dead room | Frozen hands almost touch across the landing | 12s | A repeatable visual behavior gives the song a signature |
| H6 Reverse reveal | Cold open: “I want you to stay” as the person turns | Whole sequence rewinds to “Can I be honest?” | 15s | Starting with the answer creates a loop and rewatch incentive |

## Controlled variants

For each opening, produce:

- Clean performance version with no baked text.
- One exact-caption version using only human-verified lyrics.
- One POV caption version.
- One looped ending that causally returns to frame one.

Do not change world, wardrobe, color grade, or protagonist between hook tests.
We are testing the opening—not confusing it with six different campaigns.

## Participation formats

1. **Confession reply:** “Can I be honest? I never told you…” with direct camera
   delivery and a user-selected reveal.
2. **Stay / go split:** departing-person POV, with creators cutting on their own
   choice or memory.
3. **Four-slot rewind:** glass → shirt/perfume → stair → person; creators replace
   each slot with their own relationship fragments.
4. **Duet answer:** original asks the question; response creator supplies the
   reaction during the held beat.
5. **Producer proof:** bad automated lyric → human correction → scene decision →
   final shot, demonstrating craft rather than pretending automation is magic.

## Platform cuts

- TikTok/Reels: 9–15s hook-first test units; face or physical anomaly immediately.
- YouTube Shorts: 15–30s story/process versions; evaluate Engaged views and
  completion, not raw starts alone.
- Spotify Canvas: 3–8s textless loop built from one signature physical event.
- Spotify Clips: artist context or confession story after artist profile access.
- CapCut: four replaceable creator-owned slots; no unlicensed third-party media.
- Owned site/email: full story, credits, rights transparency, and fan capture.

## Decision rule

Advance only openings that clear the campaign baseline relative to the median of
the six tests:

- Stronger 1s and 3s hold.
- Stronger Engaged-view and completion rate.
- Rewatch or loop behavior.
- Shares/saves and comments containing personal disclosure.
- Profile visit → pre-save/stream conversion.

Do not call a hook viral from views alone. Expand the top two into the flagship
edit, performance cuts, POV variants, and creator template; kill or archive the
rest with results.
