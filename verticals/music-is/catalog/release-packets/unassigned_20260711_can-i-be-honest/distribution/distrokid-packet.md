## Official constraints checked

- WAV/FLAC recommended; 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAV is typical.
- Square JPG, at least 1000×1000; 3000×3000 recommended; RGB.
- AI music must be rights-owned, non-impersonating, non-infringing, and not
  mass-generated spam.
- AI credit fields distinguish lyrics, music, all audio, and part of audio.
