# Cover Direction

Use case: music single cover and vertical promo source frame.

Creative thesis:

`Oh Chama` should read as a repaired crystalline heart over a luminous dance-floor waveform: heartbreak becoming motion, then bloom.

Prompt used:

Create a premium cover image for the song "Oh Chama" by FrankX, a glossy progressive-house track about empathy, heartbreak, and breaking then blooming. Night-black studio space with a luminous dance-floor horizon, subtle mist, and a clean low glow like stage light through haze. One central crystalline heart, visibly fractured but repairing itself with thin gold seams and small blooming light particles emerging from the cracks; tactile, expensive, emotionally vulnerable, not fantasy-generic. High-end cinematic 3D album art, polished glass/crystal material, realistic reflections, sharp poster-frame composition. Centered square composition, strong silhouette readable at thumbnail size, generous safe area around edges for cropping; no text anywhere. Dark emotional base with warm gold repair light, restrained magenta/cyan spectral highlights, euphoric but intimate. Near-black, champagne gold, restrained rose, electric cyan accents; avoid purple-blue gradient dominance. Faceted crystal, hairline fractures, liquid-light caustics, fine dust motes, subtle waveform-like floor reflections. No words, no typography, no logos, no watermark, no people, no hands, no gore.

Keep:

- Gold fracture/repair lines.
- Strong heart silhouette.
- Waveform floor glow.
- Dark negative space.

Cut:

- Extra text baked into cover.
- Generic fantasy scenery.
- Human figures or literal couples.
- Overly soft/blurred center subject.
