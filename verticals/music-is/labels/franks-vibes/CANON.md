## Sound DNA

### Genre + sub-genre
- **Primary:** Electronic
- **Sub-cohorts:**
  - Lo-fi (study/journal/evening)
  - Chill-house (evening-vibe)
  - Vibe-electronic / downtempo (creator-soundtrack)
  - Gym-electronic (workout)

### Reference triangle
1. **Tycho** — warm electronic, evening-vibe, melodic
2. **Bonobo** — organic + electronic blend, downtempo, sophisticated production
3. **HOMEWRK / Lofi Girl ecosystem** — lo-fi study aesthetic, mood-loop discipline

### Harmonic vocabulary
- Diatonic + modal mixture (warmer than pure tonal)
- Sus chords frequent
- Pad-led harmonic foundations
- Avoids: complex jazz extensions, polytonality, atonality

### Tempo bands
- **70-95 BPM** — chill / lo-fi / journal sub-cohort
- **95-115 BPM** — gym / movement sub-cohort
- **115-128 BPM** — evening-vibe / chill-house sub-cohort

### Vocal posture
- **Occasional, often chopped/processed**
- Rare clear lead vocal (when present, AI-cloned from consenting Frank-only or licensed source)
- Languageless or minimal-lyric typical
- AI-clone disclosure: required per metadata

### Production posture
- **Streaming-loudness optimized** (LUFS-target ~-14 LUFS integrated; peaks at -1 dBTP)
- Warm low-end with sub-bass presence
- Moderate compression (sub-cohort dependent)
- Deliberately lower dynamic range than Frank Riemer
- Polished but not clinical

## Visual DNA

### Color palette
- **Sunset-toned palette** — mid-saturation; warm gradients
- **Primary palette anchor:** sunset orange `#E8743C` + magenta `#D4528B` + deep purple `#3F2C5C`
- **Secondary:** aurora-like green-magenta gradients for evening sub-cohort
- **Cool sub-palette:** for journal sub-cohort — soft blue `#5B7FA8` + dusty rose `#D4A89C`
- **Mid-saturation rule:** 30-65%; brighter than Frank Riemer, less than Nona

### Typography lock
- **Display:** Geist Display
- **Body:** Geist
- **Banned:** Cinzel, Space Grotesk, Inter (per anti-pattern list)

### Cover composition rules
- **Persona-led** — each persona has a face/avatar (real or stylized)
- **Mood-context-explicit** — gym shot, sunset window, headphones-on-train, journaling-desk
- **Per-persona consistency** — same persona's cover aesthetic remains coherent across releases
- **Sunset-aurora color leaning** — ambient warmth vs. crisp daylight

- Tycho album art aesthetic (gradient-driven)
