## 🔍 In-Depth Audits

### 1. Alera — Cinematic / Mythic Voice Guardian (Arcanea Label)
* **SEO & Conflict Audit:** A post-hardcore/metalcore band named *Alera* is active (releasing EPs like *A Selfish Endeavour* in 2026). However, they operate in an entirely different sonic ecosystem. Independent platform `alera.fm` exists but has no trademark lock.
* **Analysis:** Because the existing *Alera* is post-hardcore, an elvish/cinematic/528Hz Voice Guardian named *Alera* has excellent catalog partition safety. Algorithms and search engines will separate the two based on genre-cluster profiling.
* **Verdict:** **GREEN-LIGHT**. Proceed with *Alera* as the Voice Guardian persona seed.

### 2. Lumen — Ambient Lo-Fi / Chill-House (Frank's Vibes Label)
* **SEO & Conflict Audit:** Large Russian alternative rock band *Lumen* (active since 1998, extremely prominent in Eastern Europe). Also Luca Mendoza's newer electronic project *Lumen* (debuted 2024).
* **Analysis:** Russian alt-rock *Lumen* dominates literal search strings, but operates in an entirely different market. Luca Mendoza's electronic project is small. For a Tycho-like, "light-engineered" chill-house catalog, *Lumen* is an exceptional acoustic fit. 
* **Sovereignty Play:** Release under **Lumen (Arcanea)** or **Lumen & Frank's Vibes** on DSPs to guarantee pristine algorithmic indexing.
* **Verdict:** **RECOMMENDED**. Lock *Lumen* as the official name for persona `Pulse-01`.

### 3. Aether — Chillout / Downtempo (Frank's Vibes Label)
* **SEO & Conflict Audit:** Heavily congested. Scottish chillout/downtempo producer *Aether* (Jason Taylor) has a large established footprint on Monstercat. Additionally, Berlin techno DJ/producer *Ae:ther* releases heavily on Afterlife.
* **Analysis:** Jason Taylor's Monstercat releases create a massive, high-friction overlap in our exact downtempo/chill electronic space. Indexing algorithms will struggle to distinguish a new lo-fi *Aether* from Jason Taylor's catalog.
* **Verdict:** **DEACTIVATED**. Defer or use as an album/track title, not a persona.

### 4. Dawn — Electronic / Workout (Frank's Vibes / Nona)
* **SEO & Conflict Audit:** Severe congestion. American singer-songwriter/producer **DAWN** (Dawn Richard, Danity Kane, Diddy-Dirty Money) is a global force in R&B/electronic. South Korean rapper **DAWN** (Kim Hyo-jong, former Pentagon) has millions of streams. Tony Orlando and Dawn is a 1970s pop giant.
* **Analysis:** Complete SEO black hole. Attempting to launch a new electronic persona named *Dawn* will result in severe catalog misattribution and zero discoverability.
* **Verdict:** **REJECTED**.
