### Per-Alera frequency lock — 528 Hz canon

**Every Alera release is tuned to 528 Hz.**

Specific implementation:
- **Pedal tone or drone at 528 Hz** as harmonic foundation (continuous or sectional)
- **Master tuning calibrated** so the song's tonic emphasizes 528 Hz harmonic series (e.g., A=444Hz tuning for some releases; or fundamental frequency analysis confirming 528 Hz presence)
- **Mastering reference includes spectrum analysis** confirming 528 Hz energy in the master
- **Tuning fork / sine-wave 528 Hz reference** available in studio session for performer reference

This is **non-waivable** for Alera releases. Tracks not tuned to 528 Hz cannot be Alera releases (they ship under another label/persona or stay in draft).
