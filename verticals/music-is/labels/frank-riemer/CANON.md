## Sound DNA

### Genre + sub-genre
- **Primary:** Neo-classical
- **Sub-cohorts:** solo piano, piano + strings, piano + ambient pad, occasional cello-led

### Reference triangle
1. **Olafur Arnalds** — composition restraint, dynamic-range protection, modal vocabulary
2. **Max Richter** — long-form arc, post-classical posture, score-grade
3. **Nils Frahm** — texture, piano-as-physical-object, room sound, intimate dynamics

### Harmonic vocabulary
- Modal mixture (parallel minor borrowed into major; Aeolian/Dorian flavor)
- Sus chords (sus2, sus4) common; sus-resolution as primary motion
- Occasional polytonality (2-3 bars max; resolves)
- Resolves mostly tonal; rarely fully atonal
- Avoids: jazz extensions on every chord (over-flavored), 4th-stacking (over-modern), perfect-classical voice-leading (too academic)

### Tempo bands
- 60-90 BPM typical
- 90-110 BPM rare (only for piano-led "anthem" pieces, e.g., quarterly headline single)
- Rubato common; metronomic refused except where structural

### Vocal posture
- **None primary** — solo piano or piano + strings
- Rare ambient vocal pad (wordless, processed) — disclosed in metadata as ambient-texture not lead vocal
- AI-clone disclosure: refused for this label; if vocals present, real performer (Frank himself or named consenting collaborator)

### Production posture
- **Dynamic-range protected** — mastered for film/TV sync delivery (LUFS-target ~-18 to -16 LUFS integrated; peaks below -1 dBTP)
- Warm low-end; piano felt audible
- Reverb-as-room (real or convolution; not plate-on-everything)
- No streaming-loudness compression
- Minimal digital coloration; analog warmth via tape-emulation or none

### Suno prompt anchors (5-10 fragments)

## Visual DNA

### Color palette
- **Primary:** cool grey `#2D2D2F`
- **Background:** near-black `#0A0A0A`
- **Accent (single):** warm cream `#C9A876`
- **Neutral:** off-white `#E8E5DD`
- **Banned:** any saturation above 30%; warm orange/red; bright digital colors

### Typography lock
- **Display:** Instrument Serif (editorial accent) OR Geist Display
- **Body:** Geist
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono (rare, only for technical materials)
- **Banned:** Cinzel, Space Grotesk, Inter (per Arcanea design canon anti-pattern list)

### Cover composition rules
- **No persona face on covers** — label is artist-of-record, contemplative-not-personal
- **Large negative space** (60-80% of composition)
- **Single small focal element** (hand on piano keys, single piano key, music score detail, paper texture, window light)
- **Texture authenticity** — texture-of-piano-felt, texture-of-paper-music-score, real-light not digital-painterly
- **Title placement** — bottom-third, "Frank Riemer" small top-right or bottom-right
- **Banned:** AI-fantasy-painterly, color saturation above 30%, any face, stock-photo composites
