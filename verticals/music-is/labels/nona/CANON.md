## Sound DNA

### Genre + sub-genre
- **Primary:** Punk / alt / post-punk
- **Sub-cohorts:**
  - Punk (peak-state, gym, energy)
  - Alt-rock cathartic (rebellion-context)
  - Post-punk darker (cinematic-rebellion)
  - Industrial-flavored (Phase 3+ when warranted)

### Reference triangle
1. **IDLES** — modern UK post-punk; cathartic + intelligent
2. **Fontaines D.C.** — Irish post-punk; literate + raw
3. **Early Bauhaus** — gothic-flavored post-punk foundation; atmospheric darkness

### Harmonic vocabulary
- **Power chords** as primary motion
- **Modal-based** (Aeolian, Phrygian, Dorian)
- **Occasional dissonance** for tension (parallel-perfect-fifth-led)
- **Refuses:** standard pop progressions (corporate-aligned), jazz extensions (over-flavored), perfect classical voice-leading (academic)

### Tempo bands
- **130-180 BPM typical**
- **Above 180 BPM** for hardcore peaks (rare)
- **Below 130 BPM** for cinematic-rebellion sub-cohort (post-punk darker)

### Vocal posture
- **Raw, cathartic, often shouted/screamed** — primary
- **AI-cloned Frank's voice with consent disclosure** — primary path; refuses vocal-cloning of any non-Frank artist
- **Lyrics: defiant, peak-state, anti-corporate, raw**
- **Avoids:** clean-pop-vocal posture, autotuned-perfection, corporate-marketing-aligned lyrics

### Production posture
- **Streaming-loudness optimized** but with intentional rawness preserved (LUFS-target ~-12 to -14 LUFS integrated; aggressive but not crushed)
- **Distorted guitars** — fuzz, overdrive, occasional bit-crush
- **Aggressive drums** — punchy kick, snappy snare, room-mic'd ambience
- **Raw vocal energy preserved** — minimal smoothing, minimal autotune
- **Refuses:** clinical-perfect production, corporate-pop-mix aesthetic

## Visual DNA

### Color palette
- **High-contrast, monochrome** (black `#000000` + white `#FFFFFF`)
- **Single rage-color** per release — red `#E63946` OR yellow-warning `#F4D000`
- **Rare third color** — only for cinematic-rebellion sub-cohort (deep purple `#3A1A4F`)
- **Banned:** mid-saturation soft palettes; sunset-gradient (that's Frank's Vibes); cool grey (that's Frank Riemer)

### Typography lock
- **Display:** Brutalist sans (Geist Display extended-bold weight) OR custom-distressed
- **Body:** Geist
- **Avoid:** elegant serifs (wrong canon)
- **Banned:** Cinzel, Space Grotesk, Inter

### Cover composition rules
- **Brutalist typography** — large, distressed, in-your-face
- **Xerox aesthetic** — high-contrast, photocopy-distortion textures
- **Collage-grade** — torn-paper, mixed-media, anti-design
- **Persona-led but anti-glamour** — persona depicted in raw / unposed / confrontational stance
- **Refuses:** glossy-fashion-shoot aesthetic, polished-album-cover discipline, AI-fantasy-painterly

### Reference image set
- IDLES album art aesthetic (raw, brutalist)
- Bauhaus / early post-punk album art (xerox, monochrome)
- Punk-zine collage aesthetic
- Brutalist architecture photography
- High-contrast street photography (raw, unposed)
