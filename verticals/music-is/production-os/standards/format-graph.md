# Release Format Graph — July 2026

One release world creates multiple native expressions. Do not resize one master
and call it a campaign.

## Format families

| Family | Job | Core proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `performance_identity` | Make artist recognizable | Face, voice, gaze, gesture, performance |
| `narrative_microfilm` | Make the song meaningful | Causal setup, turn and payoff |
| `confession_direct` | Create intimacy | Direct camera question or truth |
| `behind_song` | Build authorship/trust | Meaning, production or writing decision |
| `creator_template` | Enable adoption | Repeatable action/POV/caption with clean sound start |
| `fan_response` | Compound fandom | Duet, remix, cover, reaction or spotlight |
| `world_fragment` | Build canon memory | Signature object, place, rule or motif |
| `proof_live` | Establish performance truth | Live/session/one-take evidence |
| `canvas_loop` | Track-level recognition | Complete 3–8s micro-story |
| `countdown_video` | Pre-release album/EP anticipation | Eligible vertical 3–30s video |
| `full_music_video` | Flagship story/performance | 16:9 full-length video where eligible |

## Required variant fields

- Platform, surface and objective.
- Hook hypothesis and controlled variable.
- First frame and first 0.7-second event.
- Artist/unique identity cue timestamp.
- Verified hook lyric and source timecode.
- Duration, aspect, resolution, fps and safe zone.
- Shot density and causal story beats.
- Audio, captions and participation behavior.
- Remix/download permission.
- Source assets, prompt card, model/version, seed and receipt.
- Critic scores, experiment cell and status.

## Current platform corrections

- TikTok: native vertical, safe-zone aware, human and emotionally legible; test
  multiple hooks. Artist Account features support discovery and Behind the Song.
- Instagram: use Trial Reels for non-follower hook tests; inspect retention and
  replays; specify reuse/remix permissions.
- YouTube Shorts: use Engaged views, Stayed to watch, average percentage viewed,
  retention, subscribers and remixes. Raw Views include starts/replays and are
  not a cross-platform quality metric.
- Spotify: new Clips uploads ended 2026-06-17. Use Canvas; eligible Countdown
  Videos for qualifying album/EP campaigns; and full-length Spotify Video where
  access/eligibility exists. Spotify full video does not accept visualizers or
  lyric videos as the official music-video format.

## Normalized funnel

- **STOP:** 2-second rate, Stayed to watch, swipe-away.
- **HOLD:** 6-second rate, retention curve, average percentage, completion.
- **REPLAY:** replay rate or loops per viewer.
- **IDENTITY:** profile visits, follows/subscribers, branded search, recognition.
- **PARTICIPATION:** shares, remixes/uses, fan posts and saves.
- **MUSIC INTENT:** Add Song, DSP saves, playlist adds, streams/listener.
- **FANDOM:** new active, reactivated, monthly active and super listeners.

## Experiment discipline

- Minimum six materially different opening concepts before flagship spend.
- Change one major variable per cell: opening visual, first line, POV or pace.
- Keep audience, song window and world comparable.
- Inspect 2s/6s/completion and 24h/72h results where available.
- Promote the top two; archive failures with the lesson.
