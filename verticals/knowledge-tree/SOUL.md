# SOUL — Starlight Knowledge Tree

The essence that must not drift, whatever the graph grows into.

1. **Capability over consumption.** The tree measures what a person can do and prove, never what they have watched or read. If a proposed feature rewards consumption, it is off-soul.

2. **Provenance or it doesn't exist.** Every claim in the public graph carries at least one verifiable source with a persistent identifier. An unsourced node is a draft, not knowledge.

3. **The frontier is the point.** Maps of the known exist to route people to the unknown. Quest nodes — open problems — are the tree's fruit, not an appendix.

4. **Foundations first.** Mathematics, physics, life, computation, energy. Paths and applications branch from foundations; they never replace them. When in doubt about where depth goes, it goes to the trunk.

5. **Open and forkable, forever.** The canon is CC-BY, the code is MIT, the standards are cross-vendor. Anyone can fork the whole tree and grow their own. Lock-in of any kind — vendor, format, or platform — is a soul violation.

6. **Humble about what we know.** Confidence levels are explicit. "Established" is earned by replication, not assertion. The tree says "contested" and "unknown" in plain sight — that honesty is the product.

7. **Private stays private.** A person's Skill DNA — their position on the tree, their evidence, their health and identity data — belongs to them, in their vaults. The public tree holds humanity's knowledge, not any human's data.

8. **Agents serve the map; humans steer.** Agent swarms harvest, verify, and propose at scale. Humans (or human-reviewed curators) merge. No fully autonomous writes to canon.

9. **Wonder, grounded.** The pursuit — secrets of the universe, foundations of life, all knowledge there is — is stated plainly and pursued rigorously. Awe is welcome; mysticism dressed as knowledge is not.

*Built on SIP — Starlight Intelligence Protocol.*
