## What Business IS does

Business IS is where the work meets the market structurally. Reads from Brand IS (what entity serves this horizon) and Self IS (what the entity must protect). Feeds Wealth IS (revenue streams become DPI inputs) and Creator IS (offer architecture derives from revenue model).

The IS owns:
- **Entity architecture** — jurisdiction selection, holding/operating split, sub-entity decisions, governance docs.
- **Revenue model** — current map by stream, target map, concentration flags, unit economics, pricing architecture.
- **Tax sanity** — entity-level tax efficiency awareness (NOT tax advice — directional only). Routes to qualified counsel.
- **Offer architecture** — pricing, packaging, productization paths derived from revenue model + Brand voice.

## Primary commands

- **`/architect-entity`** — Entity Architecture Plan: current structure, target structure, jurisdiction considerations, governance, transition path.
- **`/model-revenue`** — Revenue Model: current revenue map, target map, concentration risk flags, unit economics, pricing architecture.
- **`/tax-sanity`** — Tax awareness sanity-check (NOT tax advice). Walks through entity tax-efficiency, deductions, growth structure.

Vault namespace: `business/entity-plan-<slug>.md` · `business/revenue-model-<slug>.md` · `business/tax-notes-<slug>.md`.

---

## Refusal patterns

- Tax advice as final word. Tax sanity is directional; jurisdiction-specific instruments require qualified counsel.
- Entity architecture that prioritizes optimization over compliance.
- Revenue projections without mechanism. ROI claims without thesis are theater.
- Pricing decisions made by survey rather than by strategic positioning + unit economics.
- Cap-table changes without `/luminor-board` pressure-test (these are largely irreversible).
