# CAPITAL — partnerships, allocators, investment posture

> How Alexandria approaches the people with capital: allocators, crypto-native whales, operators with family offices, and the cloud and model vendors whose credits lower our cost of proof. The posture is receipts first, asks second.

---

## 1. The offer to capital

Capital buys one thing from this vertical: **a private intelligence office that proves what it says.**

- **What they get:** their own Alexandria instance (private catalogue, private ledger, their vaults as native providers), weekly receipted briefs on the lines they care about, bespoke receipted deep-dives, and a Steward retainer so the office keeps running. Every claim traces to a receipt they can re-check without trusting us.
- **What we never do:** advise on positions, move money, custody keys, or let an agent touch a rail. The `payment-intelligence-system` doctrine (fail-closed, human above cap) and FrankX hard stops apply verbatim.
- **Why they would pay:** they already pay for data and still cannot trust the brief on top of it. Receipts collapse the trust cost.

## 2. Pipeline

| Stage | Entry | Artifact | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm introduction | existing network, workshop alumni, podcast guests surfaced by `particle/charts/chart` | note in private CRM | call booked |
| Discovery call | one call | their three recurring questions written as needs | plan priced via `alexandria_plan` |
| Receipted sample | one need, our budget | a signed dossier on their question | they re-check one receipt |
| 30-day pilot | signed | weekly brief + one deep-dive | `exp-2026-10-08-allocator-pilot` metric |
| Standing office | pilot metric met | Tier 1 commission + Steward retainer | quarterly review |

Falsifier: zero signed pilots from ten warm introductions means the offer is wrong for allocators; reposition to operators and founders (recorded in the experiment).

## 3. Partnership tiers

| Partner class | What we bring | What we ask | Verify before claiming |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Data catalogues** (Firecrawl Alexandria first) | curated routing, receipts, a real downstream consumer, native providers to list | listing of Starlight's public research and metrics as providers; revenue share terms if any | partner agreement in writing |
| **Model vendors** (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) | a production multi-runtime swarm with receipts, case-study material | startup / builder programme credits; managed-agent early access | programme acceptance email |
| **Cloud** (OCI, Vercel, Cloudflare, Supabase) | reference architecture, published receipts, enterprise-grade discipline | credits for Scribe batch and Forge bakeoffs | programme acceptance |
| **Distribution** (vidIQ, Postiz, podcast networks via Particle prospects) | receipted research content | sponsorship and placement | signed insertion order |
| **Payments** (Polar, Stripe, x402 ecosystem) | an agent-payments reference that is verify-only by design | merchant-of-record terms, agent-payment pilot | contract |

Rule: a partnership is listed in `MEMORY.md` only with the dated artifact that proves it. "In conversation" is not a partnership.

---

## 4. Investment posture

- Alexandria is bootstrapped from estate commissions and briefs. Outside capital is considered only when the Exchange experiment proves pass-through demand and the pilot experiment proves allocator pull; both are receipts, not decks.
- If capital is raised, it is raised against the receipt graph (a verifiable moat) and the catalogue curation, never against row volume.
- Investor materials are generated through the existing `investment-banking` skills (one-pager, teaser, datapack) from receipted metrics only.

---

## 5. Ethics line

- Whale tracking uses public on-chain and filed data through catalogued providers; no de-anonymisation, no private data brokering.
- People search (FullEnrich, PDL) is used for business introductions with a named purpose, never for mass outreach; contact enrichment is not in the catalogue.
- Attribution strings required by providers (Similarweb) ride every receipt and every brief.
