## What Second Brain IS does

Second Brain IS is the memory layer underneath every other IS. Cross-cutting because capture must be daily — miss a week and the compounding curve resets. Reads from Self IS (vocabulary fingerprint guides distillation). Feeds Brand IS (emerging patterns reshape 3-year view), Creator IS (distilled frameworks become content pillars), Business IS (captured market signal).

The IS owns:
- **Daily capture ritual** — under 10 minutes; routes captures to correct vault namespace.
- **Weekly orchestration** — clears inbox, distills emerging patterns.
- **Monthly distillation** — extracts frameworks that recurred ≥3 times.
- **Markdown vault canonical** — Mem0/Graphiti are derived indices, never source of truth. If they corrupt, regenerate from the vault.
- **Cross-IS feed** — surfaces signal that other ISes can't see by capturing what wasn't asked.

## Primary commands

- **`/capture-daily`** — under 10 min daily capture routine. Routes to correct vault namespace on first pass.
- **`/distill-insights`** — distill captures from a window into named frameworks. Applies ≥3-occurrence threshold.
- **`/orchestrate-brain`** — weekly 30-min review. Inbox clear + lightweight distillation + vault hygiene.

Vault namespace: `second-brain/inbox/` · `second-brain/distilled/` · `second-brain/frameworks/`.

---

## Refusal patterns

- Treating Mem0 / Graphiti / Notion / Obsidian as source of truth. Markdown vault is canonical; everything else is derived index.
- Capture friction that costs more than 10 min/day. Friction is the silent killer of compounding memory.
- Distillation that elevates one-time observations to framework status (the ≥3-occurrence rule is non-negotiable).
- Public vaults containing instance-private capture content (PII, partner names, financial detail).
- Tools that can't export to plain markdown. Vendor lock-in collapses sovereignty.
