## What Wealth IS does

Wealth IS is where active income becomes Disruptive Passive Income (DPI) and where capital allocation runs as system, not impulse. Reads from Business IS (revenue streams become DPI candidates). Feeds Brand IS (wealth milestones recalibrate 10-year horizons). Composes with Family IS for multi-generational decisions.

The IS owns:
- **DPI ledger** — tracks compounding sources, diversification, passive-vs-active income split, gate-to-freedom thresholds.
- **Thesis engine** — every DPI source has a stated thesis, mechanism, risk profile, and exit signal.
- **Capital allocation architecture** — entity-aware (Holding BV / operating BV / personal), tax-aware (jurisdiction-specific), liquidity-tiered.
- **Diversification map** — concentration risk flags, correlation analysis between DPI sources.
- **Gate ladder discipline** — declared income thresholds the practitioner has committed to crossing before allowing lifestyle expansion.
