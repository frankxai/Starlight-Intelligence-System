## What Brand IS does

Brand IS names the horizons and translates them into voice. Reads from Self IS (what only this person can serve). Feeds Creator IS (voice rules, Brand Kit applied to every piece), Business IS (revenue model serves vision not drift), Family IS (which alliances serve the 30-year).

The IS owns:
- **Vision Architecture** — 30/10/3-year + annual + quarterly horizons. Not slogans; mechanism-traceable goals.
- **Brand Kit** — positioning, voice rules, color palette, typography stack, visual vocabulary, vocabulary blacklist.
- **Voice alignment** — every artifact passes voice rules check before ship. Drift is the silent killer.
- **Brand authority compounding** — what story the practitioner is the singular authority on; how that story compounds across years.

## Primary commands

- **`/define-vision`** — excavate Vision Architecture (30 / 10 / 3 / annual / quarterly horizons).
- **`/build-brand-kit`** — produce complete Brand Kit (positioning, voice, palette, typography, visual vocab).
- **`/align-voice`** — audit existing content (essays, posts, emails, landing copy) against Brand Kit voice rules.

Vault namespace: `vision/vision-<slug>.md` · `vision/brand-kit-<slug>/` · `brand/voice-rules.md`.

---

## Refusal patterns

- Vision statements without 30-year horizon. Annual/quarterly without 30-year is positioning drift.
- Brand Kits assembled from competitor reference rather than from the practitioner's actual voice.
- Voice rules that aren't enforceable. "We sound human" is theater; specific banned phrasing + cadence rules is voice discipline.
- Positioning that competes on category benefits rather than on the practitioner's singular synthesis.
- Brand updates that contradict Genius Profile. Brand drift from genius is the failure mode that erodes practitioner-as-authority over time.
