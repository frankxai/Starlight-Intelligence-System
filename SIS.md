# SIS — Starlight Intelligence System

**One substrate. Many verticals. Sovereign at every layer.**

## Premise

Intelligence systems are not products. They are substrates — the load-bearing layer on which many verticals compose. SIS is the substrate. SIP is the contract that keeps the composition coherent. Every vertical that adopts SIS gains the substrate's leverage without surrendering sovereignty.

## What SIS provides

- **File contract** — canonical names and shapes for skills, agents, memory, canon, soul (`SIP.md` § 1).
- **Attestation protocol** — verifiable "Built on SIP" attribution (`SIP.md` § 2).
- **MCP registry standard** — how servers declare, compose, version (`SIP.md` § 3).
- **Command taxonomy** — protocol / alliance / vertical / sovereign layers (`SIP.md` § 4).
- **Sovereignty + attribution clause** — the social contract (`SIP.md` § 5).
- **Archetype extension** — optional canon adoption (`SIP.md` § 6).
- **Recommended sovereign stack** — default choices, not mandates (`STACK.md`).
- **Alliance forging method** — how multiple parties compose under SIS (`ALLIANCE.md`).
- **Claw suite** — installable operational units that make the substrate act (`CLAWS.md`).

## What SIS does not provide

- **Content.** Verticals own their content.
- **Canon.** Arcanea owns Arcanea canon. Wealth IS owns Wealth IS playbooks. No vertical inherits another's canon.
- **Brand.** SIS is a substrate, not a brand identity. Verticals brand themselves.
- **ICP.** Each vertical defines its own ideal customer or audience.
- **Opinions on how you live.** Wellbeing and consciousness practices sit at the *founder* layer (see `FrankX` vertical), never imposed on adopters.

## Vertical shape

Every vertical is an instance of the same pattern:

```
<vertical-name>/
├── SIS-instance.md        (this vertical's positioning + domain)
├── SKILL.md               (how AI adopts this vertical's behavior)
├── AGENTS.md              (voices in this vertical)
├── MEMORY.md              (state)
├── CANON.md               (optional — vertical-specific archetypes)
├── .claude/commands/      (vertical-scoped slash commands)
└── .<vertical>/           (vertical-specific config, e.g. .arcanea/)
```

Verticals adopt SIP by carrying the file contract and emitting `/sip-attest` on every shipped artifact.

## Composition rules

1. **No vertical owns another.** Composition is opt-in both directions.
2. **Canon is imported, not merged.** A vertical using Arcanea canon imports with attribution; it does not fork and rename.
3. **Every composition is attestation-carrying.** "Built on SIP + Arcanea canon + Creator IS playbook" is the full block. No silent composition.
4. **Verticals can go dark.** A vertical that goes inactive remains registered but carries `status: dormant`. Attribution history is immutable.

## Why a substrate, not a product

Products are replaced. Substrates are built upon. The bet: the protocol + attribution layer compounds across every vertical, every alliance, every partner that adopts it — and the canon vertical (Arcanea) compounds separately as content IP. Two compounding curves, one architecture.
