# Compose Domain Pack

## Outcome

Produce one implementable domain-pack brief that advances a defined learner from a real starting condition to a portable artifact and contribution path.

## Portfolio laws

- Default to an installable pack under an existing house. Propose a new top-level brand only when buyer, promise, distribution, steward base, and risk boundary are all distinct.
- Keep observed, derived, and hypothesized claims separate. A plausible persona or price is not validated demand.
- Maintain one named revenue validation bet at a time; place other packs in Next or Later.
- Keep modules, missions, rubrics, local or BYOK execution, export, and equal credential eligibility free.
- Price managed capacity: hosting, private tenancy, integrations, compute, observability, group review availability, and service guarantees.
- Do not invent partnerships, learner enrollment, competence, revenue, or institutional authority.

## Inputs

- Expertise, role, or learner problem
- Existing brand or house and adjacent packs
- Inspectable sources and reachable learner habitats
- Learner stage, constraints, and desired identity
- Available community and managed-service capacity

## Method

1. Write a truth ledger: what is observed, what is derived, and what remains a hypothesis.
2. Name one learner moment and specify stage, age band as descriptive rather than exclusionary, current habitat, aspiration, and friction.
3. Define the identity promise, complete free outcome, three to five proposed modules, one artifact-producing mission, and assessment or review boundary.
4. Design the community experience around contribution, critique, stewardship, accessibility, moderation, and an exportable learner record.
5. Add a paid-capacity hypothesis without changing curriculum, rubric, or credential threshold. State why this learner or institution might pay.
6. Assign the pack to Now, Next, or Later. Now requires a source-backed public path or the single named revenue validation bet.
7. Define activation or graduation evidence: repeated learner problem, sustained activity, artifact completion, peer contribution, independent stewards, and passed accessibility, privacy, license, and authority review.

## Output

Return a compact pack record with identity, promise, persona, habitat sources, academic experience, community experience, proposed modules, free outcome, managed capacity, pricing hypothesis, skill dependencies, lane, status, claim states, and unresolved decisions.

## Stop conditions

Stop and return a draft when sources are missing, a free route cannot be sustained, the proposed offer creates hidden educational advantage, or acceptance would require publication, pricing, contracting, enrollment, credential, payment, or permission authority.
