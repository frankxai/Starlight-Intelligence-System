# Plan Institutional Adoption

## Outcome

Produce an adoption brief that augments one real program or workflow through open interfaces and local capability rather than requiring wholesale platform replacement.

## Inputs

- Institution, program, learner population, and named decision owner
- Existing curriculum or workflow, systems, identity, data, assessment, and governance
- Accessibility, privacy, security, procurement, legal, and service constraints
- Desired capability, artifact, and adoption outcome

## Method

1. Map the current state: local academic or operational owners, learning outcomes, artifacts, decision rights, systems, data boundaries, and pain points.
2. Choose one bounded program or workflow. Define what Academy adds, what remains local, and what is explicitly excluded.
3. Map open interfaces for capabilities, missions, artifacts, review, consented Passport export, plugins, and local or BYOK execution.
4. Preserve institutional and learner authority: named assessors decide competence; learners own export and consent; agents remain draft-only; consequential changes require authorized humans.
5. Design the implementation boundary: tenancy, identity, approved integrations, data minimization, retention, accessibility, observability, rollback, support, and vendor exit.
6. Enable local facilitators and stewards before expanding. Publish reusable methods and adapters when licenses, privacy, and procurement allow.
7. Define a price and service hypothesis tied to managed capacity, plus measures for learning quality, access, adoption, workload, system reliability, and Commons contribution.
8. Provide stop, revise, and scale conditions. Do not represent a proposal as a partnership or signed commitment.

## Output

Return the current-state map, bounded adoption thesis, interface map, responsibility and authority matrix, learner rights, integration and exit plan, facilitator plan, service and price hypothesis, measurement model, risks, and 30/60/90-day decision points.
