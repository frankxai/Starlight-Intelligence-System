# Govern Managed Capacity

## Outcome

Define a financially legible managed service that can support the Commons without creating a hidden educational advantage or autonomous financial authority.

## Invariants

- All modules, missions, rubrics, local or BYOK execution, learner export, and equal credential eligibility remain free.
- Paid tiers may add managed compute, storage, private tenancy, integrations, observability, service guarantees, and bounded group review availability.
- Ultra is an operational-capacity tier, not a standing one-to-one relationship.
- Agents may explain or recommend a published option. They may not invent or change price, discount, debt, credit, refund policy, credential state, or buyer intent.

## Method

1. Estimate the scarce resource: compute, storage, external API cost, facilitation hours, support load, integration work, reliability, and privacy obligations.
2. Define Commons, managed individual, small-cell, Ultra, and institution boundaries only where real cost or service difference exists.
3. Publish a versioned price book with currency, tax posture, included capacity, overage behavior, renewal, cancellation, refund, and change-notice rules.
4. Keep payment deterministic: explicit buyer selection and confirmation, provider execution against the ratified price book, signed entitlement receipt, and auditable reconciliation.
5. Separate learning state, credential state, entitlement state, and payment state. Never let one silently mutate another.
6. Propose a transparent Commons subsidy allocation from managed contribution margin and require periodic human ratification before allocation.
7. Define capacity, margin, access, support, refund, and subsidy measures. Mark all unobserved prices and demand as hypotheses.

## Output

Return a tier table, cost drivers, price hypotheses, entitlement map, deterministic payment sequence, authority matrix, receipt fields, cross-subsidy rule, measurements, and unresolved legal or tax decisions.

## Stop conditions

Stop before implementing checkout when the price book, merchant authority, taxes, refunds, privacy, security, or explicit confirmation flow is unresolved.
