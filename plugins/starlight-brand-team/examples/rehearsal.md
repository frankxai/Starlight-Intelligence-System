# Reviewed post


> A strategy meeting can finish with a shared slide deck and several different ideas about what happens next.
>
> Before the conversation closes, make a short record:
>
> - What did we decide?
> - Who owns the next step?
> - What changes next?
> - What remains unresolved?
>
> Give each answer enough detail that someone can act on it. If ownership is unclear, keep the question visible instead of treating it as settled.
>
> Bring the record to the next meeting. Check what changed and which questions still need an answer.
>
> For your next strategy discussion, write the four answers before you leave. Use the record to check whether the next step has an owner and a clear action.
