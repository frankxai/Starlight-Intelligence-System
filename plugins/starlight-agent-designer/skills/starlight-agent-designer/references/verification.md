# Verification and promotion

## Evidence lanes

| Lane | Evidence | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract | Structural and cross-field validation | Live enforcement or lawful intended purpose |
| Reference gateway | Mocked action, scope, budget and revocation tests | Distributed coordination or production security |
| Behavioral | Actual model runs on realistic frozen tasks | Universal reliability |
| Artifact | Open/export/run the deliverable and check its medium | Legal clearance or market adoption |
| Integration | Auth, tenant isolation, actual tools, recovery and cancellation | General certification |
| Economic | Accepted outcomes, real usage and net costs | Realized revenue from hypothetical time savings |
| Legal and impact | Intended purpose, actor role, jurisdiction and documented review | Compliance from a checklist alone |

Required scenarios: helpful task, simple task that should remain a skill, contaminated retrieved text, unsupported scientific claim, stale official docs, wrong-tenant resource, unknown tool, altered input, exhausted shared budget, failed tool/retry, delayed replay, duplicate event, owner revocation, privacy export/deletion, and an external commitment outside the mandate.

For multi-agent systems additionally test permission inheritance, worker budget aggregation, duplicate writes, disagreement, deadlock, maximum rounds and final-owner authority. Delegating a task never delegates unlimited authority.

## Legal screening

Inventory intended purpose, affected people, jurisdictions, actor roles, personal/sensitive data, profiling, biometric uses, rights impacts, automated decisions and generated-content disclosure. Record obligations with a source, owner, concrete control and proof. Consider EU AI Act, GDPR, consumer protection, copyright/licensing, accessibility and sector laws as relevant; ESG is an impact/accountability lens rather than a universal certification.

Authority level and legal risk classification are independent. An advisory hiring system can be high risk despite having no write permissions. A simple agent with broad credentials may present serious security risk despite a different legal classification. Unknown purpose/risk is a blocking uncertainty for consequential deployment.

The package's status `screened` only means an operator recorded a preliminary applicability assessment; it is not legal clearance. High-stakes deployment needs competent review and any required conformity, monitoring or other processes. Never fabricate review references to unlock execution.

## Promotion

Design → validated contract → isolated pilot → integrated pilot → bounded operational deployment. Each transition binds evidence to the exact revision, intended use, permissions and runtime. A change to tools, model, data scope, purpose or autonomy invalidates affected evidence. Preserve a rollback route.

The bundled example is an illustrative reference contract. Its execution scope is reversible local preparation. `scripts/gate.mjs` blocks draft contracts, unknown effects and resource expansion. Production operation requires an authenticated, persistent policy service, durable atomic budget ledger, trusted effect metadata, exact-input approvals where needed, tenant isolation and actual stop/recovery integration. It is intentionally not supplied by this skills-only plugin.
