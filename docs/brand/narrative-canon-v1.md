**Status:** proposed public narrative, 2026-09-23. **Evidence baseline:** `origin/main` at `a54cc7090f53ebe4f79765ba01e6a459d835b2dd`. ## Category and promise

**One sentence:** Starlight is local-first intelligence infrastructure for owner-directed portfolios of ventures, designed to preserve knowledge, coordinate work, and make proven capabilities reusable while each venture keeps its own authority.

**Brand thesis:** Intelligence should compound.

**Product thesis, still unproven across ventures:** A verified capability created in one venture should help another venture reach a better outcome or reach it with less reinvention, without making either venture dependent on a central operator. A transferable file alone is not the outcome. The requirement is a record of the capability, permission, verification, destination, baseline, and observed effect. [Decision memo](next-era-decision-v1.md); [claim map](claim-evidence-2026-09-23.md).

**Enemy:** lost intelligence. Decisions become inaccessible; context is fragmented across tools; teams repeat work; useful practices remain trapped in projects; histories become dependent on a vendor or a single person. These are the problems the architecture is meant to reduce, not measured improvements already achieved. [Issue #197](https://github.com/frankxai/Starlight-Intelligence-System/issues/197); [strategic vault](../../memory/vaults/strategic-vault.md).

**Brand promise:** Keep consequential knowledge and work inspectable, portable, and useful to the next team. The current reference build provides some local primitives for this promise; an integrated portfolio outcome is not yet demonstrated. [Claim map](claim-evidence-2026-09-23.md).

## The story at four lengths

### Ten seconds

Starlight is local-first intelligence infrastructure for venture portfolios. It helps preserve knowledge and verify reusable capabilities so the next venture can begin with better context. The cross-venture outcome is the thesis we are testing.

### Thirty seconds

Most ventures lose useful intelligence between tools, people, and projects. Starlight brings memory, work, capabilities, and evidence into an owner-directed operating model. Local memory and verification tools run today; Foundry has passing skill-package paths but incomplete plugin projection, while portfolio command and transfer remain staged. The test is whether a proven capability from one venture measurably improves the next while both remain independent. [Claim map](claim-evidence-2026-09-23.md).

### Sixty seconds · founder voice draft

Across Starlight, FrankX, and Arcanea, the work spans systems architecture, making things, and operating distinct ventures. The repeated problem is that valuable decisions and methods are easy to lose as work moves between conversations, tools, and teams. Starlight is an attempt to give that intelligence a durable form: memory with provenance, capabilities that can be inspected, work with named ownership, and evidence that can challenge our claims. Some of those parts run locally now; the integrated portfolio loop is still a build and a measurement problem. The ambition is simple to state and hard to earn: venture N+1 should start more capable because venture N existed. Each venture must still be able to stand on its own. The longer horizon asks what knowledge deserves to survive the software and organizations that first held it. [CLAUDE.md voice and role contract](../../CLAUDE.md); [decision memo](next-era-decision-v1.md); [claim map](claim-evidence-2026-09-23.md).

This is a proposed first-person script for founder review, not a recorded quote or a biographical account. Do not add a childhood sky story, a single moment of revelation, or a claimed career event without the founder's source and approval.

### Three minutes · narrative script

People have long looked to the stars for orientation. Light is information arriving across distance and time. The name Starlight draws from those two observations: orientation and persistence. The repository's [star-wisdom essay](../../site/content/cosmos/star-wisdom.md) develops that editorial frame. It is a reason for the name, not product evidence.

Today, an enormous amount of useful work is recorded in fragile places: a conversation nobody can retrieve, a decision without its reasons, a workflow that works in one project but cannot be carried into another. A team may build the same capability again while a working version sits elsewhere. The cost is not just storage loss. It is the loss of an organization's ability to learn.

Our thesis is that intelligence should compound. A venture should produce its own product and knowledge, then be able to contribute a proven method to the rest of an owner-directed portfolio. The next venture should inherit what has been checked, with provenance and permission, while retaining its own decisions and identity. That is the proposed economic and architectural mechanism; it still needs outcome evidence. [Decision memo](next-era-decision-v1.md); [claim map](claim-evidence-2026-09-23.md).

Starlight is the infrastructure we are building for that mechanism. Local memory stores and retrieves records. Foundry skill-package paths work, while its OpenAI plugin projection is blocked by an audited-main toolchain digest mismatch. SIP conformance and signed-receipt tools provide verification paths. Work and portfolio models exist, but an operating Command Center and automatic, measured transfer across ventures are not yet proven end to end. A local test is evidence for its local contract, never evidence that a venture improved. [Claim map](claim-evidence-2026-09-23.md).

The portfolio is a constellation because the ventures remain distinct. FrankX is the founder and public operating surface; Arcanea develops imaginative worlds and cultural work under its own canon; other ventures can make their own products and decisions. Starlight can provide shared infrastructure without taking their authority. Public Starlight Notes are a proposed durable place for carefully curated knowledge and values. Private vault material has a separate boundary. [Constitution](../../site/src/app/constitution/page.tsx); [strategic vault](../../memory/vaults/strategic-vault.md); [decision memo](next-era-decision-v1.md).

The distant ambition is to help useful intelligence endure through changing tools, teams, companies, and perhaps generations. Better scientific and civic capability and a flourishing civilization are horizon commitments, not current features or promised outcomes. The next honest step is smaller: show the code, show the receipts, measure whether transfer actually helps, and allow a venture to leave with what it owns. [Horizon vault](../../memory/vaults/horizon-vault.md); [claim map](claim-evidence-2026-09-23.md).

## Mission and vision

**Mission:** Build owner-directed infrastructure that preserves consequential knowledge, makes work and claims verifiable, and helps proven capabilities travel between independent ventures.

**Long-horizon vision:** Useful intelligence survives changes in software, teams, and organizations, contributing over generations to more capable, caring, and sustainable human institutions. Scientific discovery and humanity's future beyond Earth can motivate the horizon; neither is a Starlight product promise. [Horizon vault](../../memory/vaults/horizon-vault.md); [constitution](../../site/src/app/constitution/page.tsx).

**Near-term decision rule:** Prefer a working local primitive, a reproducible proof, and a portable record over an expansive claim. [Board pre-pass](../boards/2026-09-23-next-era-public-narrative-prepass.md).

## Metaphor grammar and terminology

**Use celestial language to explain orientation, distance, time, and the relation between independent ventures.** A venture can be a star; a portfolio can be a constellation; light can represent knowledge traveling. In product controls, say **Venture, Decision, Work, Memory, Team, Capability, Evidence**. A status, permission, owner, and source must remain literal. [Decision memo](next-era-decision-v1.md); [CREATOR.md](../../CREATOR.md).

**Evidence Mode:** exact nouns, dates, source paths, statuses, and uncertainty. **Horizon Mode:** editorial scale and wonder, always marked as long-term intent. Both use the same technical precision. Visual derivation belongs to [design-coherence](../../skills/vision/design-coherence.md) and the implementation design system; the experimental [Queen visual skill](../../skills/vision/queen-swarms-visual.md) can inform motion composition without turning its prior generated imagery into proof.

**Terms with fixed meanings:**

- **SIP:** Starlight Intelligence Protocol. Attestation declares composition with specified elements; signing and verification are separate operations. [SIP](../../SIP.md); [protocol tools](../../protocol/README.md).
- **Sovereignty:** an owner retains decision rights and can use, fork, or leave with its own records and capabilities. Treat export independence as a test to demonstrate, not a blanket current guarantee. [STACK.md](../../STACK.md); [decision memo](next-era-decision-v1.md).
- **Capability:** a versioned practice, skill, workflow, or software unit that can be explained, checked, and reused. A prompt copied between repos is only a candidate. [Foundry code](../../tools/foundry/lib/); [decision memo](next-era-decision-v1.md).
- **Receipt:** a verifiable record of a specific operation or result, with source and scope. A design mock or footer is not a receipt. [Protocol tools](../../protocol/README.md); [claim map](claim-evidence-2026-09-23.md).
- **Compounding:** verified reuse that improves a later venture's outcome against a baseline. No complete qualifying record exists in current main. [Claim map](claim-evidence-2026-09-23.md).

**Prohibited shortcuts:** “AI civilization,” “self-evolving empire,” “fully autonomous venture factory,” “the network is live,” “universal protocol interoperability,” “frictionless sovereignty,” “100-year memory guarantee,” “planet-scale operating system,” invented numeric proof, fake partner logos, and mystical Arcanea terms on a Starlight product screen. Avoid random nebulae, astronauts, glowing brains, meaningless circuits, and synthetic microtype as substitutes for exact diagrams. [CREATOR.md](../../CREATOR.md); [Board pre-pass](../boards/2026-09-23-next-era-public-narrative-prepass.md); [claim map](claim-evidence-2026-09-23.md).

## Copy tests

1. **Comprehension:** In the first screen, can a new visitor name the category, the operating problem, what runs locally now, and the path to proof?
2. **Status:** Does each sentence about a capability carry or link to LIVE, STAGED, RESEARCH, or HORIZON status?
3. **Receipt:** Could a developer find the code, test, deployment check, or outcome record that supports the exact wording?
4. **Sovereignty:** Can each venture retain decision rights and leave with its own knowledge, subject to an actual export test?
5. **Voice:** Does the sentence sound direct, technical, warm, and specific rather than institutional or mythic? [CREATOR.md](../../CREATOR.md); [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md).
6. **Horizon:** Would the sentence still be true if network federation and autonomous operation never ship? If not, mark it as a hypothesis or aspiration.

**Reject:** “Our autonomous constellation learns from every venture and powers the next generation of civilization.” It asserts three unproven outcomes and hides the product.

**Use:** “The local reference build can store memory, package capabilities, and verify SIP records. We are testing whether those pieces can help a later venture without taking away its independence.” [Claim map](claim-evidence-2026-09-23.md).
