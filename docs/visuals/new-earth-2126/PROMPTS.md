# New Earth 2126 — production prompt packet

## Global generation contract

- Route: built-in Codex image generation, text-to-image.
- Use case: `stylized-concept` with photorealistic naturalism.
- Output role: final art candidate and future-film style frame.
- Master surface: wide cinematic landscape, composed for a 16:9 crop with important subjects away from extreme edges.
- Cohesion: civic biophilic realism, clear institutional daylight, warm mineral materials, natural weather, subtle film grain, physically plausible scale.
- Recurring mark: one thin oxidized-red physical provenance line integrated into real infrastructure.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, emblems, signage, captions, borders, watermarks, or interface overlays.
- No living-artist imitation, franchise shorthand, or derivative character/architecture language.
- No dark science-fiction cockpit, purple-blue fog, cyan neon, glowing orb, portal, node cloud, humanoid robot, floating hologram, flying-car swarm, sterile glass megacity, pasted-on vertical gardens, or generic solarpunk collage.
- People and animals must have believable anatomy, natural posture, varied ages and appearances, and non-staged interactions.
- Artificial intelligences are plural, non-anthropomorphic, accountable systems expressed as distinct architectures, instruments, habitats, and environmental services.

## 01 — The first light

**Purpose:** opening key art; establish the whole civilization at massive scale.

**Emotional core:** lucid hope grounded in stewardship.

**Prompt:**

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: opening cinematic style frame, wide landscape master composed for 16:9
Primary request: New Earth in the year 2126 at first light, an immense flourishing coastal watershed civilization seen from a high natural escarpment after rain.
Scene and subject: the primary subject is the living watershed itself. In one coherent view, native mountain forest feeds braided rivers and restored wetlands; the water passes through a dense but low-rise terraced city and opens into mangrove islands and a clear ocean. In the immediate foreground, an elder engineer and a young ecological apprentice stand in wet native grasses beside a quiet field instrument, giving human scale. Below them, ordinary people walk, farm, repair transit, and care for public gardens. Long wildlife corridors visibly connect mountain, city, and coast. Renewable energy appears as climate-specific roofs, shade canopies, distant offshore structures, and elegant storage integrated into real buildings, never as spectacle. Three different great artificial intelligences are implied through distinct civic infrastructures: a watershed observatory, a public archive, and an adaptive coastal protection system; none is humanoid.
Composition: monumental deep landscape with readable foreground, middle-distance civic life, and ocean horizon; physically plausible routes and water flow; generous sky; strong scale without clutter. A thin oxidized-red provenance line appears as the edge of a public maintenance path descending through the landscape.
Art direction: photoreal architectural and environmental visualization with documentary humanity; large-format 35mm feeling; crisp natural atmosphere, wet limestone, pale timber, white ceramic, weathered aluminum, linen, real soil and foliage; restrained color grade; subtle film grain; morning sun breaking through layered clouds; detailed but not hyper-sharpened.
Brand behavior: Civic Intelligence — calm, accountable, public-minded, human and ecologically literate. Advanced systems are visible through clean water, connected habitats, safe public space, and people doing meaningful work.
Avoid: text or symbols; dark sci-fi; neon; glossy needle towers; flying cars; empty utopian plazas; vegetation pasted onto glass; impossible topography; generic fantasy; overprocessed CGI; cloned people; malformed hands or faces; artificial symmetry; watermark.
Output role: final art candidate, poster-quality still, crop-safe for wide editorial use.
```
