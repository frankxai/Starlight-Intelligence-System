## Reusable quality controls

Apply these to every future cinematic generation:

- Use one approved identity anchor and only the minimum supporting reference images.
- State exactly which identity, garment, object, pose relationship, and material must remain unchanged.
- Define a story beat with pressure, action, consequence, and handoff.
- Name the lens, camera height, blocking, depth strategy, motivated sources, highlight behavior, grain, and color science.
- Describe seams, closures, articulation, weight, drape, weathering, and performer movement.
- Make the instrument separate from the body and show its stop or rollback behavior.
- Forbid text inside generated pixels; apply exact notation after generation.
- Reject generic power poses, poster lineups, armor drift, uniform drift, and unmotivated glow.
- Compare the output beside the identity anchor at thumbnail and full resolution before promotion.
- Store the final prompt, source references, asset hash, review decision, and known defects.
