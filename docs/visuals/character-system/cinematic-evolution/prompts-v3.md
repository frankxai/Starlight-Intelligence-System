## V3 quality law

Future character prompts must answer six questions before aesthetic language begins:

1. What responsibility must the viewer understand?
2. What one silhouette event belongs only to this character?
3. What human-scale instrument physically performs the work?
4. What gesture makes its operation legible without text?
5. Where is the stop, uncertainty, rollback, or incomplete judgment visible?
6. How do costume construction, material, color, and movement reinforce that same answer?

If outfit, instrument, and action tell different stories, the design restarts regardless of rendering quality.
