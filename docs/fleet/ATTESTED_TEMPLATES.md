# Starlight Attested Fleet Templates

> Curated and attested fleet configurations for sovereign operators.
> Built on SIP — Fleet Architecture Tier.

---

## 1. Overview
Instead of configuring dozens of individual agent profiles from scratch, builders instantiate pre-attested fleet templates. Each template bundles:
- A curated subset of the 144 registered agents
- Memory vault schema mappings and decay parameters
- Autonomic single-operator orchestration (Starlight Queen)
- Required thin overlays (observability, routing, or middleware)

---

## 2. Attested Fleet Templates

### 1. Solopreneur Founder Fleet (`template_solopreneur_founder`)
- **Focus:** Complete technical and business operations for a one-person software or services company.
- **Leadership Council:** Orchestrator (routing), Prime (decisions), Architect (systems), Sentinel (security).
- **Domain Modules:** Business IS (revenue & entity modeling), Visionary IS (design & voice coherence).
- **Durability Guarantee:** Tier 1 Local + Tier 3 Blockchain-Anchored Horizon/Decision notes.

### 2. Creative Media & Content Fleet (`template_creative_media_studio`)
- **Focus:** High-velocity multimodal content production (video, kinetic typography, audio, social distribution).
- **Core Agents:** Envoy (intake), Weaver (creative synthesis), Social Visual Director (cinematics), Composition & Production (audio/music).
- **Thin Overlays:** OpenRouter (text), Fal.ai / Runway (media rendering).

### 3. Embodied Robot & Physical Fleet (`template_embodied_robotics`)
- **Focus:** Autonomous physical robotics, drones, and quadrupeds.
- **Actuator Middleware:** ROS2 / Zenoh thin overlay.
- **Safety Protocol:** Fail-closed Benevolent Emergency Stop. Actuator halts immediately upon hazard zone boundary infraction.
