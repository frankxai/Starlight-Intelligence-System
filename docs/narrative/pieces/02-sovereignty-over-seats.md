# Sovereignty Over Seats: Why Solopreneurs Reject Enterprise AI Rents

> Public Piece 2 — Starlight Systems Series
> Attestation: Built on SIP

The enterprise software pricing model is predicated on a single metric: the **seat**.
You pay $30/month for every employee who logs in.

When you are a solo builder commanding 50 AI agents, the per-seat model becomes an absurdity. If you had to pay $30 per agent seat per month, your software bill would be $1,500/month before running a single inference token.

Enterprise software vendors want you to believe that "teams" are the only unit of production.
They are wrong.

**The Solopreneur Reality:**
- One human operator owns the vision, the aesthetic taste, and the capital.
- The "team" is a fleet of specialized AI runtimes running on local hardware and cheap commodity inference APIs.
- Your intelligence stack should run local-first, cost pennies on the dollar, and belong entirely to you.

Starlight is free and open-source (MIT). We don't charge per seat. You own your disk, your models, and your memory. When you want sovereign durability or fleet mesh coordination, you buy modular packs or lifetime support — never an amnesiac subscription.
