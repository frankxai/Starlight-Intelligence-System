# Academy Commons Charter

**Status:** proposed for human ratification  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-29

## Founding law

> Knowledge, capability and portability are free. Capacity, context and accountability may be paid.

The Academy Commons is the product, not a lead magnet. Evidence remains mandatory for consequential capability and credential claims; payment never becomes evidence and never unlocks a different standard.

## Complete public path

Every published Academy Pack must provide, without payment:

1. the capability graph and prerequisites;
2. the complete curriculum and missions for at least one capability path;
3. source skills, plugin manifests, reference agent definitions, schemas, rubrics, evaluators, public fixtures, and a local runner;
4. local or bring-your-own-key execution;
5. artifact and Evidence Bundle creation;
6. access to the same assessment contract and credential-eligibility standard;
7. learner-controlled Passport export; and
8. a documented contribution path.

Hosted inference and human review may have transparent quotas or queues. A quota may slow a free learner; it may not make completion, export, or credential eligibility impossible. No learner is required to surrender evidence rights, publicize private work, or contribute upstream to finish a path.

## One quality standard

- The curriculum, rubric, evaluator contract, credential meaning, appeal path, and public verification semantics are identical across public, sponsored, institutional, and managed service modes.
- Payment may improve turnaround time, compute capacity, privacy, isolation, integration, observability, reliability, and service accountability.
- Payment may not purchase hidden curriculum, easier assessment, stronger credential meaning, favorable evaluator discretion, public ranking, ownership of learner evidence, or governance control.
- Automated and peer feedback may be formative. A consequential human credential still requires an authorized human decision and an independent appeal path.

## Optional managed services

Managed service tiers may provide private workspaces, reserved compute, higher concurrency, private memory, managed agents, backups, integrations, institutional tenancy, data residency, pooled Guardian Councils, calibrated review queues, and SLAs. Ultra is a system experience, not an exception channel: it carries no default founder-call or bespoke-curriculum entitlement.

Managed services are activated only after open adoption demonstrates a recurring scarce-capacity or accountability need. Pricing and any Commons funding allocation remain separate ratification decisions; they are not part of the learning contract.

## Three graphs, three authorities

```text
Capability graph:
competency -> skill -> mission -> artifact -> evidence -> assessment -> capability -> opportunity

Execution graph:
objective -> typed task -> executor binding -> state -> checkpoint -> recovery -> proof

Resource graph:
principal -> mandate -> resource envelope -> consumption -> receipt -> reconciliation
```

These graphs may reference one another only through explicit, typed, policy-checked edges. Capability truth never depends on a resource entitlement. Resource consumption never confers capability, credential, reputation, ownership, or contribution status.

## Agent and payment boundary

An agent may consume allowlisted compute, models, datasets, or services only under a revocable mandate, explicit resource envelope, idempotency key, immutable receipt, beneficiary disclosure, and hard cap. It may not create debt, accept legal terms, open credit, change prices, define revenue share, manage payroll, custody learner funds, transact in speculative assets, release disputed payouts, or purchase from a related party without human ratification.

No agent may merge a canonical graph, publish private learner evidence, issue or revoke a human credential, change an assessment rubric, activate a paid service, or accept residual risk. Experimental persistent agents remain inactive until their security, behavioral, drift, memory, necessity, and termination evidence lanes pass.

## Open release and promotion gates

1. **Release gate:** contracts, provenance, license, privacy, security, accessibility, conformance fixtures, and claims review pass.
2. **External-use gate:** ten independent learners install the pack, five complete Mission Zero, three consented artifacts are produced, and two external contributions are accepted.
3. **Repeatability gate:** three independent groups reuse at least 70% of the workflow and two non-founder maintainers can operate it.
4. **Sustainability gate:** only then may optional managed or institutional services graduate from hypothesis to active offer.

Revenue may prove demand for managed operations. It is never permission to learn, build, prove, export, or contribute.

## Anti-capture rules

- Sponsors and institutions cannot buy curriculum control, governance votes, exclusive skill domains, favorable credentials, or exclusive reusable IP.
- Learner evidence is learner-controlled and private by default. No advertising, resale, or model training without granular opt-in.
- Institutional customization uses overlays and adapters; reusable primitives return upstream where licensing and consent permit.
- The public path cannot be degraded to manufacture conversion.
- Credentials, privacy, assessment, and open-access changes require a public decision record and named human authority.
- No single funding source should become a silent constitutional authority; concentration thresholds require an explicit independence review.

## Licensing boundary

The current repository license is MIT and remains authoritative for repository code unless a human-approved legal change is made. Curriculum and other content need an explicit content-license decision and provenance register before broad release. No automated process may silently relicense existing work.
