# workspace-subscription-hypothesis

Status: proposed

## Context

The paid value to test is reliable shared coordination and recovery. Supplier inference and runtime costs vary by mission and must remain attributable.

## Options

- Fixed workspace subscription with separately funded compute
- Bundled unlimited execution subscription
- Self-managed open core only

## Decision

Fixed workspace subscription with separately funded compute

## Consequences

Proposed commercial shape only; no price, checkout change or customer charge is authorized here. Preserve BYOK/self-managed open core. Record supplier cost, failed attempts, review/support and payment fees. Before breadth expansion, require three of five pilot teams to complete two accepted missions weekly for three weeks, reduced reconstruction/review time without completion-quality loss against the assembled baseline, and two paid renewals.

## Revisit

Missed repeat-use, quality, time-saving or renewal threshold; stop expansion and repair the workflow. Unknown contribution margin prevents scaling paid compute.

Evidence: owner-direction
