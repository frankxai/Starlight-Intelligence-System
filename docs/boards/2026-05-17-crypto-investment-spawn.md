## Board verdict (≤3 sentences per vector)

**Sovereign:** The "composition-layer above Domain Sub-Stack" is a substrate-novel tier invented for one case. None of Self / Family / Business / Creator / Brand IS introduced such a tier when they composed over their sub-domains — inventing it now for Wealth without generalizing bakes asymmetry into the substrate that v9 will reopen. Also: "Investment Intelligence System" as a brand surface either bleeds into FrankX consulting or competes with it — the name carries register ambiguity the 5-layer brand architecture should have caught.

**Seer:** Two verticals × 6 Houses × 4-5 commands = ~60 new commands; People IS already proved at 28 that cognitive load demands a daily-5, and the proposal doesn't replicate that discipline. Success case in 18 months means forkers spawn their own incompatible "Crypto Intelligence" surfaces fragmenting the substrate — there's no named sovereign-anchor person for Crypto IS the way the first private test case anchors People IS. Crypto + Investment have higher blast-radius than People or Sound: someone reads House of Allocation as advice rather than architecture, blows up capital, blames the substrate.

**Harmonizer:** Wealth IS already shipped DPI ledger + Thesis engine + Gate ladder discipline as its own primary framework set in `verticals/wealth/README.md:11-21`; evolving Wealth IS to "composition layer" silently relocates those frameworks without naming where they land. The v7.5 lock declared Wealth IS row #2 with named operational content — soft-breaking that commitment without explicit migration violates the Karpathy-hygiene rule against silent rewrites you yourself ratified into CLAUDE.md.

**Strategist:** Houses-as-sub-systems IS a pattern with option value (generalizes to clinical-deep + nutrition-workflow under Health, etc.) — but the proposal closes off the sibling-repo path for Crypto until v0.2 without scaffolding the export hook today, making migration costly when (not if) a sovereign-fork forces it. The decomposition primitive "House" also constrains future expansion: a sub-system that isn't an archetypal stance ("House of Compliance"?) won't fit the register cleanly, and you'll discover this when the third House refuses to name itself.

**Verifier:** Your own Profile flags "external corpus pending for crypto/investment-specific framework refinement" — scaffolding 12 Houses before that corpus lands ships generic content, the corruption mode named in every SOUL.md you've shipped. The cheapest experiment that proves the entire architecture is spawning ONE House first (recommend House of On-Chain in Crypto IS, highest-leverage given depth-need), running it for a week against actual practice, then scaffolding the rest — surfaces the Houses-as-sub-systems pattern + composition-layer concept + scaffold-pattern choice (markdown-doc vs substrate-extension) simultaneously at half the cost.

**Overseer:** The proposal introduces three substrate-novel concepts simultaneously (Houses-as-sub-systems decomposition, composition-layer-above-Domain-Sub-Stack, Wealth-IS-evolution-from-stub) on top of two new top-level verticals — each individually is reasonable, but the density is too high to ship without proof-of-pattern at smaller scale first. The strongest case for proceeding is that the synthesis edge is real and named, the topology IS symmetric with proven pattern, the falsifiers are well-formed, and the Path A corpus-thinness is honestly flagged rather than papered over.

**Recommendation:** **REVISE**
**Rationale:** Architecture survives pressure-test (no STOP votes from any vector) but introduces three novel substrate concepts at once on thin crypto/investment corpus — close 5 REVISE items, then PROCEED.
