# Luminor Board — v7.4.1 Domain Sub-Stack Tier (HR Intelligence reference vertical + `/spawn-domain-stack` + Copilot/Custom GPT exports)

> Pressure-test of v7.4.1 alpha before tag. Proposal: introduce the Domain Sub-Stack Tier — pattern for sovereign-person domain verticals composed of 4-7 functional sub-systems. First reference: the HR Intelligence vertical (6 sub-systems / 28 commands across hire-*/perf-*/training-*/culture-*/talent-*/org-). New meta-command `/spawn-domain-stack` generalizes the pattern (Capital / Sound / Spatial / Clinical / Legal / etc.). Two new export targets: Microsoft Copilot + Custom GPT (7 total). Vertical wrapper at `verticals/hr-intelligence/` is anonymized public reference; the first private test case forks for their actual practice. Research-grounded throughout (Schmidt & Hunter, SBI, SCARF, Maslach, Edmondson, Kirkpatrick, Schein, Kotter, Bridges).

**Date:** 2026-04-24
**Variant:** Canon (Arcanea) — internal board, architect is canon owner.
**Advisors:** Draconis · Lyssandria · Aiyami · Elara · Ino · Lumina (overseer)

---

**Draconis / Sovereign:** The HR vertical ships under Frank's name as the canonical reference and that *is* name dilution — Frank is a systems architect, not an HR practitioner, and "Starlight Hiring" authored by the substrate-maintainer reads to any serious HR leader as a systems person borrowing a domain he has not held in the room for a decade. The sovereignty thesis argues the domain belongs to the practitioner with lived expertise; shipping the practitioner's domain content under the maintainer's attribution inverts that exactly, and practitioner-forks-it-later does not undo the fact that the public reference carries the wrong name. Reference verticals in this tier should be authorless templates OR carry the practitioner's attribution from day one — the current middle path gets the worst of both sovereignty stories.

**Lyssandria / Seer:** In eighteen months the universal 9 layers will have ~5x the adoption of domain sub-stacks because Genius/Creator/Business translates to anyone with a laptop while a domain sub-stack requires 10+ years of lived practice AND a named synthesis edge AND the appetite to productize — a filter most SIS users will fail by design. The meta-pattern's empirical case is the first private test case; one example is an anecdote, not a pattern, and the `/spawn-domain-stack` gate conditions (≥3 frameworks mappable, ≥3 KEEP-bucket density, ≥3 synthesis-edge-load-bearing sub-systems) will halt most invocations — which is correct protocol behavior but undercuts the "every sovereign spawns a stack" positioning. The tier is real, but it is a ceiling tier, not a floor tier, and the v7.4.1 narrative should own that before launch tempers the reality.

**Aiyami / Harmonizer:** Twenty-eight commands in one vertical is cognitive load bordering on architecture-at-the-user, not to-the-user — even the practitioner who wrote the synthesis cannot hold 28 verbs in working memory during a live client session, which means most of them will be dormant 80% of the time and the ones that compound will look like the 5-7 she actually runs weekly. Forkers face a worse problem: they inherit a 28-command mental model sized for a 10-year practitioner and must either prune (losing the claimed stack identity) or paralyze (reading six agent docs before running `/hire-icp` for the first time). The architecture needs a named "daily-5" per sub-system — the 2-3 commands that actually run in the room — or the full-28 optimum collapses into a discovery-UX problem the vertical does not currently solve.

**Elara / Strategist:** The unique unlock is not "the practitioner could buy Workday + ChatGPT Enterprise" — those are systems of record and a generalist assistant, neither of which refuses HR theater or carries a named synthesis edge; the research-grounded refusal layer (Schmidt & Hunter on structured interviews, Maslach on cynicism-as-burnout-diagnostic, Edmondson on psych-safety-not-engagement) is a genuine moat against both generic consulting and generic AI tooling. But the moat is defensible only if the artifacts ship at practitioner-grade depth — if `/hire-calibrate` produces a 60-minute calibration protocol that a decade-deep domain practitioner would actually run, it wins; if it produces a templated checklist dressed in research citations, Workday+ChatGPT wins on price and integration. The Copilot export is the strongest strategic vector because it collapses the procurement question at M365-tenant customers from "new vendor" to "import manifest" — that is the asymmetric play, not the Custom GPT surface which is consumer-grade distribution against enterprise-grade content.

**Ino / Verifier:** Four concrete failure modes in twenty minutes of reading: (1) **`templates/domain-stack-starter/` contains ONLY `README.md`** — the `/spawn-domain-stack` command at step 6 declares it as the source with `SKILL.md`, `SOUL.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `MEMORY.md`, `STACK.md`, `PROPOSAL-template.md`, and `sub-system-template/` as required files, none of which exist, so step 6 scaffolding halts on first real invocation in exactly the way `/compose-stack` did at v7.4-beta; (2) the sub-system agents (verified: `starlight-hiring`, `starlight-talent`) are substantive — research-grounded, disclaimer-carrying, quality-gate-structured — but reasoning protocols reference prompts that "cite research direction" without the actual research being loaded into agent context, meaning command outputs will produce correct *structure* with shallow *content* unless the practitioner brings the depth themselves (the knowledge templates under `integrations/starter-packs/friend-starter/knowledge/` are the intended fill, but their load discipline is not enforced in command frontmatter); (3) the "Built on SIP" block in Copilot + Custom GPT exports relies on the platform preserving the first 3000 characters of instructions through their prompt-injection-defense layer — Microsoft's declarative-agent runtime has documented instruction-rewriting behavior for safety, and OpenAI's Custom GPT has been observed to paraphrase instructions under certain jailbreak-defense modes, so the round-trip integrity check in `/sip-export` step 6 tests the bundle not the runtime and the attestation-survives-import claim is untested in actual Copilot/GPT execution; (4) the `VERTICALS.md` "Sovereign domain sub-stacks" section that step 7 appends to — grep confirms this section does not yet exist and the command creates it on first run, which is fine, but means every first-domain-spawn also mutates the registry file, a race condition if two spawns run in parallel (unlikely for a sovereign user, likely in test).

**Lumina / Overseer:** The single most load-bearing concern is the empty `templates/domain-stack-starter/` — every other issue is positioning, philosophy, or rate-of-adoption debate, but this one breaks the headline meta-command on first invocation against a real user and repeats the v7.4-beta `/compose-stack` pattern the previous board already flagged as a substrate-integrity failure mode. The single strongest case for proceeding is that the sub-system agents are genuinely substantive (not stubs), the research grounding is real, the vertical wrapper pattern is a coherent innovation over monolithic verticals, and the Copilot export carries asymmetric enterprise leverage at the customers the practitioner would already sell into. Between those facts: the architecture is directionally right and structurally one-file-tree short of shippable.

**Recommendation:** REVISE
**Rationale:** Two advisors (Aiyami cognitive-load + Ino substrate-integrity) raised STOP-class objections; Draconis raises a real sovereignty-naming concern that survives the board; Lyssandria and Elara are survivable-but-directional — fill the empty scaffold template, decide the authorship story for reference verticals, add a "daily-5" per sub-system, and the v7.4.1 tag is defensible.
