# Direction 02 — Celestial Orrery

**Thesis:** Turn the Academy graph into a spatial observatory whose semantic layers orbit one competency.

- **Composition:** concentric 3D capability orbits and four spatial execution stations, with a sparse screen-space HUD and floating selected-object plate.
- **Type:** Newsreader for chamber names, Inter for transcript copy, JetBrains Mono for coordinates and authority state.
- **Imagery:** R3F geometry, restrained bloom, material depth, and schema-backed light threads; no raster narrative art.
- **Interaction:** orbit, zoom, click-to-focus, and a guided camera trace through the proof path.
- **Motion:** slow semantic orbits and camera recentering; reduced motion freezes the scene and replaces flight with discrete selection.
- **Responsive:** 3D on capable desktop hardware; static radial SVG on mobile and low-power devices.

**Rejection reason:** spatial spectacle weakens exact relation-label reading, raises accessibility and fallback cost, and conflicts with the operational-page rule that reserves R3F for the homepage.
