# Academy Graph Observatory — System-shape decision

**Selected direction:** Proof Loom.

The Observatory exists to make a bounded claim inspectable: can a learner choose and defend the smallest governed execution topology without activating a persistent agent or hiding a consequential decision?

Proof Loom wins because the product differentiator is not a graph aesthetic. It is the visible separation of capability, execution, evidence, and authority. A deterministic SVG/DOM projection preserves typed edges and complete semantic equivalents while supporting one earned motion sequence: Mission Zero advances through skill and independent verification, then stops at the accountable human.

The first public release is fixture-backed and read-only at `/academy/graphs`. It does not claim a live Academy runtime, completed learner evidence, active agents, issued credentials, educational efficacy, or managed commerce. Hypothetical evidence and Passport projections remain labeled previews until canonical records exist.
