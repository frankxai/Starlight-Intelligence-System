## Anti-pattern: skipping steps

**Service → Template → Community → Platform → Ecosystem.** Each step exists because:

- Without **service** experience, you don't know which patterns to template.
- Without **templates**, your community has nothing to gather around.
- Without **community**, your platform is a tool, not a movement.
- Without **platform**, your community caps at how much human attention you can give.

Skipping straight to platform produces a generic SaaS that nobody hires. Wait until Tier 1-3 are crystallized enough that the platform's correctness is obvious.

## Constraints

- **Frank's brother (PV-Lager) does NOT want to become support desk.** The AI offering must shift responsibility AWAY from the operator toward structured self-service, installer enablement, and buyer clarity. Reflected in the PV-Lager pilot's specific design.
- **Time is the bottleneck, not capacity.** Frank's hours are the limiting reagent. Tier 1 sprint at €7,500 monetizes those hours; later tiers monetize their crystallization.
- **Sovereignty clause holds.** Even Tier 4 platform must let operators export everything. Attribution-via-Built-on-SIP is the only compounding mechanism, never lock-in.
