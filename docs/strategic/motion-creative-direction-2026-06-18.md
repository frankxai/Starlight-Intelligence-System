## Root cause of bad execution

We conflated **two viewers** and **two surfaces**:

| Surface | Wrong (P0) | Right |
|---------|------------|-------|
| **Homepage hero** | Estate Factory architecture (vaults, /si, Mind/Mesh) | Protocol promise: persistent + sovereign |
| **Download / estate** | Doc slides with internal labels | Commissioning story: voice → army → proof |

The motion **plan** listed both as P0 week-1 without separating **message hierarchy**. Infrastructure shipped; **creative strategy did not**.

---

## Strategic split (non-negotiable)

```
Homepage loop     →  Cold builder  →  "Memory stays. You own it."     →  Ambient, no copy
Factory scroll    →  Warm lead     →  "One voice in. Army out."       →  Narrative, sparse copy
Receipt card      →  Skeptic       →  "Mechanical proof, not claims." →  JSON → seal motion
```

One excellent asset per viewer beats three mediocre generic videos.

---

## Why motion still matters

1. **Homepage** — H1 is strong; `BrainHero` is static SVG. A **slow connection pulse** makes "persistent context" felt in 3s muted — conversion to quickstart/protocol.
2. **Estate commercial** — €25k+ sale needs **90s trust** without a sales call. Hero-demo narrative exists in docs; video makes it legible to non-readers.
3. **Factory** — Repeatable brief → storyboard → frame → render. Process v2 gates prevent another commit-before-eyes.
