Each page should explain the buyer, result, deliverables, proof, prerequisites,

every reader to an unrelated homepage. Preserve working URLs when improving copy.

## A small campaign

Audience: [specific people with the problem]
Authorized channel: [your site, opted-in newsletter or permitted community]
Observation window: [start/end; suggested first test: 14 days]
Paid-media limit: EUR 0 unless the owner separately authorizes spending.

Create these assets from the same verified example:

1. Tutorial: show the input, the steps, actual output and limits; link to the sample.
2. Repository README: explain first use, prerequisites, license and canonical page.
3. Short demonstration: record the real task with captions and no private data.
4. Announcement draft: name the problem, show what exists and link to the example.

Example announcement structure:

> I made [artifact] for [buyer/task]. Here is a sample of [actual output].
> It includes [specific files] and requires [prerequisites]. Try the first task:
> [canonical URL]. Tell me where the instructions stop being clear.

Drafts are not sent automatically. Obtain the appropriate authority before
publishing or contacting anyone. Do not manufacture endorsements or urgency.

## Campaign links

Use consistent UTM values on links you control. Example (replace the destination):
https://example.com/product?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=first-task

Keep canonical metadata on the clean URL. Do not put personal identifiers,
customer names, credentials or private project details in URLs. Record campaign
names in your own table. UTMs label visits; they do not install analytics.

## Evidence and decision

Record aggregate page visits, sample downloads, first-task completions, delivery
failures and consented questions. Analytics must be configured and verified before
you claim it measures anything. If unavailable, label counts unknown.
