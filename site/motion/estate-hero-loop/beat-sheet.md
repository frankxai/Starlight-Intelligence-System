---

## Why this direction (design conviction)

P0 failed because it was **category error**: we made explainer slides for a surface that must be **atmospheric proof**. June 2026 premium is not darker gradients — it is **material honesty** (glass refracts), **brand continuity** (same ring as live `BrainHero`), and **one physics metaphor** (incoherent beams → coherent wave = amplification).

We do not need Frank to choose between A, B, and C from a menu. The homepage loop is **A+B**: lens at the orchestrator hub, ring agents unchanged, motion upgrades from decorative pulse to **phase sync**.

## Timeline

| Time | Beat | Motion | Viewer read |
|------|------|--------|-------------|
| 0.0–2.0s | **Siloed** | Filaments pulse independently; ring wave desynchronized; lens dim | Agents alone, weak |
| 2.0–5.0s | **Couple** | Filaments bend toward lens; ring wave tightens | Substrate engaging |
| 5.0–7.5s | **Sync** | All ring nodes peak together; lens brightens; rim flash | Phase lock |
| 7.5–8.5s | **Amplify** | Output beam blooms; optional ring expansion pulse | Combined power |
| 8.5–10.0s | **Seam** | Beam decays; phases drift apart → rhymes with 0.0s | Loop invisible |

**Curve:** `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` — site `--ease`. No snappy eases.
