# Intelligence in Service of Life — release direction

## Experience thesis

The recipient is a builder, creator, educator, parent, or public-interest leader who already senses that advanced intelligence changes the distribution of power. The prior state is abstract agreement with “AI for everyone” without a usable standard. The observed truth is that access alone does not preserve agency. The proposition is that a constitution can become a product operating system. The consequence is a portfolio that can be examined through concrete tests: authorship, portability, contestability, capability gained, and stewardship.

## Three directions considered

1. **Prismatic protocol — selected.** A precise Starlight document where one constitutional signal refracts into distinct operating ventures. It fits the existing protocol site, preserves the evidence-led first principle, and gives the doctrine weight without imitating a government site.
2. **Civic ledger.** A dense, versioned specification with compliance tables above the fold. Strong for governance audiences; too narrow as the portfolio’s public constitution.
3. **Living futures cinema.** Full-bleed imagery and scroll choreography showing possible futures. Emotionally expansive; too dependent on generated media for a document that must first earn trust through language and tests.

The selected direction is intentionally still. The prism is a coded diagram, not ambient decoration. Existing site navigation and reduced-motion behavior remain intact.

## Portfolio rule

Starlight owns the doctrine and sovereign substrate. FrankX interprets it under a named human voice. GenCreator operationalizes creator agency. Arcanea operationalizes imagination and cultural possibility. The pages share claims and links, never duplicate bodies of copy.
