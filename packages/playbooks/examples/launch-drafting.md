# Build an editable founder launch kit

Use the supplied founder brief to produce concrete, editable drafts. Keep the
customer, problem, offer and next action visible. Identify assumptions and avoid
inventing customer evidence, revenue, testimonials or performance results.
Documents are draft artifacts. Publication requires the host's separate approval.

This example uses fixed local templates. It does not perform market research or
model inference. Replace its tool adapter with an authenticated runtime adapter
only after enforcing resource resolution, priced admission and durable effects.
