## Integrity rules

- **Silent composition is a breach.** If canon is adopted, every artifact says so.
- **Sovereignty clause applies.** The vertical owner retains full ownership of their canon extensions. Starlight has no ownership claim.
- **License conflicts halt composition.** If your domain's license intent (e.g., commercial use) conflicts with adopted canon's license (e.g., CC-BY-NC), decline composition or negotiate license upgrade upstream — do not silently breach.
- **Canon changes upstream propagate.** If adopted canon version-bumps in breaking ways, this vertical's canon declaration is re-reviewed at the next cycle close.

## Why most Domain Sub-Stacks decline

Domain sub-stacks (HR Intelligence, Capital Intelligence, Clinical Intelligence, etc.) are instrument-based — their value is the structured protocol the sovereign's lived practice produces. Canon adoption adds composition overhead without adding signal. The synthesis edge does the work. Canon is for narrative domains, world-bound practices, and creative verticals where shared aesthetic substrate is the load-bearing asset.

If unsure: `decline`. You can always compose later; unwinding silent composition is expensive.
