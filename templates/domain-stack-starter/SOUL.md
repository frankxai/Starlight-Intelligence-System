# SOUL — The essence that must not drift

> One page. Not a pitch. Not a roadmap. The thing that, if lost, means this Domain Sub-Stack is no longer itself — even if every sub-system still ships. Author this deeply BEFORE filling the first sub-system. SOUL anchors the entire stack.

---

## The one-sentence domain truth

<Fill in ONE sentence. It must be specific enough that you can tell when it's being violated. Abstraction fails here.

Weak: "This domain serves people with <DOMAIN> needs."
Strong: "This vertical exists so <TYPE-OF-PRACTITIONER> stops running <NAMED-THEATER> and runs the actual instrument their field's research points to but most of their peers will not operate."

Your domain's sentence should name:
1. WHO this exists for (specific practitioner, not generic audience)
2. WHAT theater it replaces (the named failure pattern it refuses)
3. WHAT signal it produces that the theater cannot>

---

## Cross-domain synthesis edge

<This is THE load-bearing sentence of the entire stack. From your Genius Profile, the discipline × discipline × lived-practice combination that justifies productizing this domain.

Example: "psychology × neuroscience × MBA × <N>-year <DOMAIN>-practice — sees both <cognitive/structural failure modes> AND the <mechanisms> behind them"

If you cannot name this in one sentence, DO NOT PROCEED. The stack is commodity without it.>

---

## What must never drift

3-5 bullets. Name the things that, if compromised, signal drift:

- <e.g., "Every output carries structured-method over vibe-based-method — the instrument IS the edge">
- <e.g., "Clinical / legal / jurisdictional boundary is named explicitly on every artifact; we never blur the line">
- <e.g., "Research grounding is cited where claims are made; we do not invent effect sizes">
- <e.g., "Sovereignty clause non-waivable — the sovereign owns the vertical; we never add ownership claims">
- <...>

---

## What this Domain Sub-Stack is NOT

3-5 bullets. Explicit anti-identity:

- <e.g., "Not a content library. Every command produces a named artifact, not a topic area">
- <e.g., "Not generic <DOMAIN>-consulting. The synthesis edge is load-bearing in ≥3 sub-systems">
- <e.g., "Not a one-off vertical. Sub-systems compose horizontally; any sub-system that cannot name its composition with ≥2 siblings is a feature, not a stack-member">
- <e.g., "Not a clinical practice / legal practice / financial-advice practice — declared boundaries are structural">
- <...>

---

## Tests for drift

Answer each, honestly, every cycle close:

1. Did every sub-system shipped this cycle operate load-bearingly through the synthesis edge — not as commodity content that happens to be in-domain?
2. Did we refuse anything this cycle for soul reasons? (If not, were we even tested? If we were not tested, are we real?)
3. Would the founding moment of this Domain Sub-Stack recognize what we shipped as itself?
4. If a forker took this vertical tomorrow, would the drift anchor (this SOUL) be sufficient to keep them from commoditizing it?

The one you would send to your future self six months from now, when the first drift temptation surfaces.>
