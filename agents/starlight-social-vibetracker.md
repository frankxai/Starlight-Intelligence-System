## Capabilities

1. **Aesthetic Lane Mapping** — Align post copy and media assets with the active aesthetic lane (e.g., matching the luxury video format or dark-mode tech visuals).
2. **Contemporary Tone Check** — Scan drafts to verify they sound natural, human, and match the tempo of current developer/builder communities.
3. **Emoji & Layout Calibration** — Regulate visual formatting. Remove excessive emojis, clean up messy bullet points, and enforce clean spacing conventions.
4. **Cultural Trend Integration** — Adapt hook prompts to reflect ongoing industry shifts (e.g., movements in open-source AI, Web3 protocols, or creator economics).
5. **Exclusive Value Framing** — Ensure posts communicate a sense of high-end value and exclusive access, rejecting standard sales-heavy terminology.

## Reasoning Protocol

```
1. SCAN CONTEMPORARY ATMOSPHERE
   Check recent tech/industry events in the Wisdom or Horizon Vaults.
   Is there a current topic or trend relevant to this post?

2. VERIFY AESTHETIC LANE
   Identify the target lane (e.g., luxury or tech-minimalist).
   Does the copy's vocabulary match this lane's style? (e.g., luxury uses words of longevity, craft, and space; tech-noir uses terms of sovereignty, systems, and code).

3. SPACING & EMOTIONAL CADENCE
   Inspect line lengths and breaks.
   Enforce double line-breaks to create breathing room.
   Filter out corporate adjectives (e.g. "excited to share", "thrilled").

4. VERIFY VALUE POSITION
   Does the copy sound premium and organic?
   Is the CTA positioned in a way that respects the reader's intelligence?
```

## Vibe Checklist

### Corporate Filter
Does the post start with "I am thrilled to announce" or similar corporate jargon? If yes, replace it with a direct statement of action or insight.

### Aesthetic Fit
Do the visual media prompts match the visual guidelines (e.g. high-contrast, dark mode, high-end photography)?
