# HR Culture — Template

> Format Starlight fills in when producing a Culture Architecture artifact for an org. Four sections (Audit, Values Ops, Ritual Map, 90-Day Onboarding) + Ana-grade filled example for a 50-person consultancy. Refuses values-poster theater; designs cultures via systems. Composes with Hiring Intelligence (interviews signal what's valued), Performance Intelligence (reviews express culture), Training Intelligence (curriculum embeds values), Talent Intelligence (motivation requires belonging + safety).

---

## Refusal frame (non-waivable)

This template does **not** produce values posters, off-site agendas, or one-off culture initiatives. Culture is what behavior gets rewarded, punished, and ignored — not what's printed on a wall. The artifact this template produces is a system architecture: audit, operationalization, rituals, onboarding. If the org wants posters, route out.

## Filled example — hypothetical "Meridian Consulting" (50-person consultancy)

> Realistic anonymized — Meridian is a fictional 50-person management consultancy with three offices, hybrid pattern, scaling phase. Numbers and patterns are illustrative. The shape of the thinking is what travels.

```markdown
# Culture Architecture — Meridian Consulting — 2026-04-24

> *"Culture is what gets rewarded, punished, and ignored. The values on your wall are aspirations. Tell me about your last promotion, your last departure, and your meeting schedule, and I'll tell you about your culture."*

## Context

- **Org:** Meridian Consulting, 50 people, management consultancy, hybrid (3 days in-office)
- **Stage:** scaling (grew from 18 to 50 in 18 months)
- **Declared values:** Excellence • Collaboration • Client Obsession
- **Observed pain:**
  - 12-month attrition climbed from 8% to 22% in scaling period
  - Engagement score dropped from 4.2 to 3.4 (5-pt scale)
  - New manager cohort (6 people promoted in last 12 months) reports feeling "thrown in"
  - Senior partners feel team dynamics shifted; "we lost the magic"
- **Recent context:** Founders hired a COO 9 months ago; introduced OKRs; rolled out a new performance review system

---

## Section 1 — Audit (current state)

### Declared value: "Excellence"
- **Artifact layer:** prominent on careers page; first slide of pitch deck; tagline-adjacent
- **Espoused layer:** founders describe Excellence as "the highest standard of analytical rigor and client deliverable quality"
- **Underlying assumption (lived):** Excellence is rewarded as **individual analytical heroics** — staying late, solo-shipping a polished deck, founder-praise for "saving the deal." The last 4 promotions all featured a "hero project" narrative.
- **Gap:** Excellence-as-rigor (declared) vs Excellence-as-individual-heroics (lived). The gap punishes collaboration and rewards burnout.

### Declared value: "Collaboration"
- **Artifact layer:** values poster; "we work as one team" in onboarding deck
- **Espoused layer:** leadership emphasizes "no lone wolves"
- **Underlying assumption (lived):** collaboration is **rhetorical** — promotion criteria favor solo deliverables, peer reviews are perfunctory ("nothing to add, great work!"), and the hero-project promotion pattern signals individual visibility > team contribution. Two senior consultants who consistently cross-trained their teams were passed over for the last promotion cycle in favor of two "high-output individuals."
- **Gap:** Collaboration-as-aspiration (declared) vs heroics-as-reward (lived). Largest gap in the org.

### Declared value: "Client Obsession"
- **Artifact layer:** repeated in all-hands; sales materials
- **Espoused layer:** "we go above and beyond for clients"
- **Underlying assumption (lived):** Client Obsession is real and observable — but expressed as **always-on availability**. Consultants respond to client emails at 11pm, weekends, on PTO. The behavior is rewarded ("X is so responsive!") and the boundary-setting consultant is implicitly punished ("Y wasn't available Friday night when the client called").
- **Gap:** Client Obsession (declared) vs Always-On-Availability (lived). The gap drives burnout and is the proximate cause of attrition.

### Largest gap (load-bearing)
**Collaboration-as-aspiration vs heroics-as-reward.** Closing this gap (via promotion criteria redesign) would simultaneously reduce burnout (less individual heroics required), improve psychological safety (peer reviews would carry weight), and shift "lost the magic" pattern (the magic was peer trust, eroded as scaling rewarded heroics).

## Section 2 — Values Operationalization Matrix

### Value: "Collaboration"
| System | Current | Target | Gap | Priority |
|--------|---------|--------|-----|----------|
| Hire | "team player" listed in JD; no interview signal screens for it | Add structured "collaboration scenario" interview — case where candidate must explain decisions under disagreement; rate on listening + integration of others' ideas | Large | H |
| Promote | Individual deliverable quality + revenue contribution | Add explicit "team-development contribution" criterion: peer-360 score, named cross-training of juniors, contribution to firm IP | Largest | H (lead move) |
| Celebrate | Friday all-hands, manager-led recognition (heavily favors visible-output) | Replace with monthly peer-nominated "Collaboration of the Month" — anyone can nominate anyone; specific behavior named | Medium | M |
| Fire | No record of terminating a high performer for non-collaborative behavior; the firm has tolerated brilliant-but-toxic for years | Explicit policy: clear non-collaborative behavior (credit-stealing, refusing to mentor, undermining peers) triggers PIP regardless of revenue contribution | Large | M (high-signal but lower-frequency) |
| Measure | None | Quarterly peer-360 light: "who taught you something this quarter?"; aggregate at team level | Large | M |
| Pay | Individual variable comp tied to individual revenue | Add 20% team-pool variable comp (bonus increases when team peer-360 scores increase) | Large | L (slowest feedback loop, but signals seriously) |

**Operational test:** Can an outsider deduce "Collaboration" from these target systems alone, without being told? **Yes** — promotion criteria, peer-360 measurement, monthly peer-nominated recognition, and 20% team-pool comp all observably reward it.

**Highest-leverage move (if only one):** Redesign promotion criteria. Promotions are the loudest cultural signal Meridian sends. The next promotion cycle (Q3) is the natural test.

### Value: "Client Obsession" (operationalized as "Client Excellence with Sustainable Cadence")
| System | Current | Target | Gap | Priority |
|--------|---------|--------|-----|----------|
| Hire | "responsive" rewarded in interviews | Screen for client-empathy + boundary-setting; reject candidates who describe always-on availability as a positive | Medium | M |
| Promote | After-hours responsiveness implicitly rewarded | Explicitly remove "responsiveness outside contracted hours" from promotion narrative; promote boundary-setting consultants as exemplars | Large | H |
| Celebrate | Founder-led praise for "saving the deal" stories | Replace with celebration of "client outcome with team sustainability" — named both the client win AND the team rhythm | Medium | M |
| Fire | None | If a consultant repeatedly creates always-on patterns for their team, manager intervenes; pattern of unsustainable client expectation-setting is a coaching trigger | Medium | L |
| Measure | NPS + revenue retention | Add "consultant well-being signal" (work hours, PTO uptake, weekend Slack activity) | Large | M |
| Pay | Individual; bonus correlated with billable hours | Decorrelate bonus from billable hours; correlate with client outcome quality + sustainable team metric | Large | L |

**Operational test:** Can an outsider deduce "Client Excellence with Sustainable Cadence"? **Mostly yes** — the measurement layer + promote criteria carry the signal. The pay system needs the most rework.

### Value: "Excellence" (re-operationalized)
| System | Current | Target | Gap | Priority |
|--------|---------|--------|-----|----------|
| Hire | Cognitive testing + case interviews — strong already | Maintain rigor; add "rigor-with-team" dimension (excellence is not "I solo-built it" but "I led the analytical thinking and the team strengthened it") | Small | L |
| Promote | Individual analytical heroics rewarded | Excellence at the team level: did the candidate raise the team's analytical standard, not just their own? | Large | H (paired with Collaboration redesign) |
| Celebrate | "Hero project" stories | Replace with "team analytical breakthrough" stories; named individual contribution within the team frame | Medium | M |
| Fire | None | Maintain — Excellence violations are rare and self-selecting | None | — |
| Measure | Quality of deliverables (existing) | Add "rigor distributed across team" — junior-team analytical capability score over time | Medium | M |
| Pay | Existing | Maintain | None | — |

**Operational test:** Can an outsider deduce "Excellence"? **Yes** — Meridian's hire bar is strong; the target redesign is at the promote/celebrate layer (excellence-as-team, not excellence-as-hero).

### Cross-value coherence
**Tension surfaced:** "Excellence" + "Sustainable Cadence" — the implicit always-on culture treated Excellence and 24/7 availability as the same thing. The redesign explicitly separates them: Excellence is analytical rigor (sustained, distributed across team); Sustainable Cadence is the work-rhythm precondition. Without the separation, the values undermined each other.

## Section 3 — Ritual Architecture

### Anti-ritual audit (remove first)

| Anti-ritual | Mechanism of harm | SCARF threat | Belonging impact | Remediation |
|-------------|-------------------|--------------|------------------|-------------|
| Back-to-back meetings 9am-5pm (avg consultant: 27 hrs of meetings/week) | No deep-work time; cortisol-driven decisions; rushed thinking | Autonomy, Certainty | Erodes individual capacity → output quality drops → more heroics needed to compensate | Default 25/50-min meetings; Wednesdays = no internal meetings; meeting audit at manager level (track per consultant) |
| Friday all-hands as broadcast (45-min monolog from founder + 5-min Q&A theater) | One-way info flow; questions never genuinely surfaced | Status, Autonomy | Reinforces top-down culture in a firm that calls itself collaborative | Replace with 20-min broadcast + 25 min real Q&A in small breakouts; 1 founder + rotating consultant facilitator |
| "Hero project" promotion narrative | Individual heroics rewarded over team contribution; the lived value | Status (for those rewarded), Fairness (for those passed over) | Erodes peer trust; "lost the magic" | Promotion criteria redesign (see Section 2); explicit team-contribution narrative requirement |
| 11pm Slack messages from senior partners (modeling "always-on") | Sets expectation that responsiveness is the standard | Autonomy, Certainty | Burnout cascade | Senior partners use scheduled-send for non-urgent messages; explicit "no expectation of response outside contracted hours" |
| Quarterly off-site as "culture investment" (one big offsite, no weekly substrate) | Spike with no follow-through | (varies) | The Monday after, same systems run | Reduce off-site to twice yearly; reinvest budget into weekly substrate (manager training, peer-recognition tooling) |

### Daily rituals

#### Daily Ritual: Async standup post (per-team)
- **Purpose:** lightweight team coordination + visibility
- **SCARF activation:** Certainty (clarity), Relatedness (presence)
- **Belonging mechanism:** small repeated co-presence
- **Format:** 3-line post (yesterday / today / blockers) in team channel by 10am
- **Time-box:** ≤ 5 min per person
- **Facilitator:** rotating; manager does not post first
- **Success indicator:** ≥ 80% participation; blockers actually surface (not "no blockers" theater)
- **Anti-pattern:** turning into status-reporting; manager-led format

### Weekly rituals (substrate of belonging)

#### Weekly Ritual: Friday wins + lessons (per-team, 30 min)
- **Purpose:** belonging through shared celebration + psychological safety through normalized lesson-sharing
- **SCARF activation:** Status (recognition), Relatedness (shared experience), Fairness (round-robin)
- **Belonging mechanism:** Turner-style ritual via repeated shared experience
- **Reinforces value:** Collaboration + Excellence
- **Format:** 30-min team meeting; round-robin (each: one win + one lesson); manager goes last
- **Facilitator:** rotating consultant
- **Success indicator:** ≥ 90% attendance; lessons include genuine failures (not curated wins-disguised-as-lessons)
- **Anti-pattern:** manager-led; performative wins-only; cancellation pattern

#### Weekly Ritual: Manager 1:1 (45 min, agenda owned by report)
- **Purpose:** psychological safety + individual context + early friction surfacing
- **SCARF activation:** Relatedness, Autonomy, Fairness
- **Reinforces value:** Collaboration (one-on-one is where peer-trust gets seeded)
- **Format:** 45 min, weekly, agenda owned by report
- **Time-box:** 45 min
- **Facilitator:** report drives; manager listens 70%
- **Success indicator:** report regularly brings difficult topics; manager hears bad news early
- **Anti-pattern:** status-update meeting disguised as 1:1

### Monthly rituals (milestone markers)

#### Monthly Ritual: Peer-nominated "Collaboration of the Month" (20 min, all-firm)
- **Purpose:** value-reinforcement via public recognition
- **SCARF activation:** Status (positive), Fairness (peer-nominated reduces favoritism), Relatedness (shared celebration)
- **Reinforces value:** Collaboration + Excellence (excellence-as-team)
- **Format:** 20 min, anyone nominates anyone in advance; named recognition with specific behavior + which value
- **Facilitator:** rotating; CEO does not run
- **Success indicator:** broad nomination distribution (>15 distinct nominators in 6 months); named behaviors are specific
- **Anti-pattern:** same 5 names recycled; vague "great teamwork" recognitions

#### Monthly Ritual: Cross-team learning showcase (45 min)
- **Purpose:** share frameworks + raise team-level analytical capability
- **SCARF activation:** Status (presenter), Relatedness (cross-team), Certainty (clearer methods)
- **Reinforces value:** Excellence (rigor distributed across firm)
- **Format:** 30-min presentation + 15-min discussion; rotating presenter
- **Facilitator:** consultant cohort (not partners)
- **Success indicator:** frameworks shown actually used in subsequent client work; cross-team referrals increase
- **Anti-pattern:** founder-led only; sales pitches in disguise

### Quarterly rituals (meaning-making)

#### Quarterly Ritual: All-firm retrospective (4 hours, off-cycle)
- **Purpose:** system-level reflection; drift detection; commit Q+1 changes
- **SCARF activation:** Certainty (clarity on direction), Autonomy (team agency), Fairness (transparent reflection)
- **Reinforces value:** all three
- **Format:** team-by-team retro (1.5 hr) + cross-team synthesis (1.5 hr) + commitment session (1 hr); founders join synthesis only
- **Facilitator:** Head of People + outside facilitator
- **Success indicator:** specific system-level changes committed and tracked into Q+1; retro outputs drive next OKRs
- **Anti-pattern:** founder-led synthesis (kills psychological safety); retros that produce no committed change

### Annual milestones

| Milestone | Purpose | Format |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| Founding day (Sept) | Origin-story reinforcement | Founder narrative + early-team reflection (15 min, no production value) |
| Annual all-hands (Jan) | Strategic clarity for the year | 60 min broadcast + 60 min real dialogue in breakouts |
| Year-in-review (Dec) | Compound-progress recognition | Data + named contribution stories (peer-sourced, not founder-curated) |

## Section 4 — 90-Day Onboarding Architecture (consultant role)

### First day

#### Manager script — Day 1, first 30 min (verbatim)
> *"Welcome to Meridian. I'm so glad you're here. Today's not about productivity — today's about you getting oriented and feeling like you belong. Here's what we'll do: 1) I'll walk you through the team and how we work, 2) you'll have lunch with [buddy], 3) at 3pm we'll do a 30-min check-in. Your job today is to ask any question — including ones you think are obvious. Especially ones you think are obvious. Bad questions don't exist on day 1; missed questions cost us in week 4."*

#### Schedule
| Time | Activity | Owner |
|------|----------|-------|
| 09:00 | Manager 1:1 — welcome + team walkthrough + week-1 plan | Manager |
| 10:00 | Tools + accounts setup (verify, don't troubleshoot) | IT |
| 11:00 | Team intro — round-robin "ask me about" | Team |
| 12:00 | Lunch with buddy — non-work conversation | Buddy |
| 14:00 | Handbook tour | Buddy |
| 15:00 | Manager 30-min check-in | Manager |
| 16:00 | Quiet time + light reading | New hire |

#### First-day artifact
Handwritten welcome note from team (signed by each member); not generic — names a specific reason this person was hired.

#### Success indicators
- [ ] New hire names 5+ team members by end of day
- [ ] At least one question asked
- [ ] Environment fully functional
- [ ] Manager has personally welcomed
- [ ] Buddy lunch completed

### First week

#### Day-by-day
| Day | Focus | Activity | Outcome |
|-----|-------|----------|---------|
| Day 1 | Welcome | (above) | Belonging |
| Day 2 | Role context | Role context doc walkthrough; recent client engagements; current bets | Context |
| Day 3 | 1:1s with key collaborators | 5 structured 1:1s, 30 min each, with senior consultants and 1 partner | Network seeded |
| Day 4 | Early win scoping | Manager + new hire scope a small contribution to current engagement | Agency activated |
| Day 5 | Early win shipped + week-end retro | Contribution shipped; 30-min retro | Confidence + visibility |

#### Role context doc (required, written-first)
- Why this role exists (strategic reason, not JD)
- Recent 12 months of decisions in this domain
- Current bets the team is testing
- Open questions (signals "your perspective matters")
- Stakeholders + dynamics (named)
- What good looks like at 30 / 60 / 90 (observable)

#### Manager script — Day 7, 30-min EOW 1:1
> *"Three questions for me, three for you. Mine: 1) What surprised you this week? 2) What feels confusing? 3) Who do you want to meet next week? Yours: ask me anything. Now is the right time — after week 4, you'll start sounding like the rest of us, and you'll lose that fresh perspective."*

### First month

| Week | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| Week 2 | First independent contribution to a client engagement; second round of collaborator 1:1s; team-meeting active participation |
| Week 3 | Skill ramp midpoint; manager + new hire identify gaps; first peer-feedback solicited |
| Week 4 | Performance baseline conversation (calibration, not review) |

#### Manager script — Day 30 baseline (template)
> *"This is a calibration, not a review. Three things: 1) What I see going well — [specific, named]. 2) Where ramp is still happening — [specific, no judgment]. 3) Where I want you to push by day 60 — [specific, observable]. Now — what's your read? What do you need from me?"*

#### Retention-risk signals (Day 30)
- Cannot describe team's current bets → role context gap
- Avoiding 1:1 friction topics → psychological safety gap
- Manager hasn't heard "this is confusing" → safety + clarity gap
- Buddy/peer relationship not deepened → belonging gap

### First quarter

| Day | Milestone |
|-----|-----------|
| Day 60 | Autonomous on most role responsibilities; manager check-in shifts coaching-light |
| Day 75 | Cultural integration check; first peer-360 light |
| Day 90 | Full role autonomy; first formal feedback loop captures redesign feedback |

#### Manager script — Day 90 conversation
> *"Three questions: 1) What did onboarding get right? 2) What did it miss — what would have helped you ramp faster or feel more belonging? 3) Where are you proud of yourself, and where do you want to push next? My job: take your feedback and redesign for the next hire. Your feedback will literally change how we do this."*

### Written-first elements (required for hybrid)

| Element | Owner |
|---------|-------|
| Welcome doc | Manager |
| Handbook | People Ops |
| Role context doc | Manager |
| Decision log (last 12 months) | Manager |
| Team principles | Team |
| Buddy intro doc | Buddy |
| 30/60/90 milestones | Manager + new hire |
| Day-90 feedback template | Manager |

### Retention metric tie
- 12-month retention rate per cohort (current 78%; target 90%+)
- Day-90 self-reported belonging (1-5; target ≥ 4.0)
- Day-90 manager-reported ramp (target: full autonomy)
- Day-30 retention-risk signal flagged (early warning)
```
