# Vision Architecture — Template

> Format Starlight fills in when producing a Vision Architecture. Five horizons (30/10/3-year + annual + quarterly). Each horizon names a concrete artifact. Ladders are tested. Drift tests are specific to the person.

## Filled example — "Maya, product-ops consultant" (fictional composite — any resemblance to real persons is coincidental)

```
# Vision Architecture — Maya Lindqvist — 2026-04-24

## 30-year horizon — the world
A world where product operations is a design discipline, not a coordination function. Where senior leaders understand the decision architecture underneath every launch and build organizations that pay down decision debt instead of compounding it. A world where the phrase "ship it and see" has been replaced — by Maya, by the practitioners she trained, by the methodology she documented — with something that names what the work actually is: the design of choice environments where teams can make their hardest decisions on evidence without being consumed by them.

## 10-year horizon — the life
Maya lives in a walkable city (likely Copenhagen or Rotterdam). She runs a recognized practice-leading studio with 100+ trained practitioners deploying her methodology across hardware and product organizations. Her weekly time splits roughly: 30% teaching inside the studio's certification program, 30% diagnostic-level work with a small roster of product leaders (never more than 8 at a time), 20% writing (third book in progress, second edition of the first), 20% research with the two graduate fellows her studio sponsors. Annual revenue in the €2–3M range, split between studio fees, advisory roster, and licensing. Two full-time direct reports (operations lead + research lead), the rest contract or sponsored. Personal life includes long bike rides, a workshop bench at home, and a standing Friday-evening practice of sketching one object by hand.

## 3-year horizon — the state
Maya is running a 50-client consultancy — half advisory roster, half launch-diagnostic engagements. Her first book is published (trade, not academic, to keep the work accessible) and has landed in two recognizable-enough conversations to generate a steady inbound stream. She has trained 20 practitioners in her methodology, the first five of whom now deploy it inside their own engagements under her licensing framework. Team: 2 full-time (Maya + ops partner). Revenue: €600–900K.

**Named deliverables that must exist at year 3:**
1. First book published (trade press, not self-published) — "The Decision Debt Ledger" or similar working title
2. Methodology documented as a certifiable curriculum — 6-module program
3. 20 trained practitioners active under licensing
4. 50-client consultancy revenue stabilized
5. Operations partner hired and running the executor work from the DELEGATE bucket

## Annual horizon — this year's ships (2026)
1. **Launch the methodology as a product** — the 6-module certification program, first cohort Q3 — serves 3-year deliverable #2
2. **Train the first 5 practitioners** — end-of-year cohort one graduates — serves 3-year deliverable #3
3. **Book outline + 3 sample chapters** — pitched to agent by Q4 — serves 3-year deliverable #1
4. **Productize one offer** — the Launch-Readiness Audit as a standalone 6-week engagement, 10 clients — serves 3-year deliverable #4
5. **Hire operations partner** — by Q3, trained off the Freedom Path DELEGATE bucket — serves 3-year deliverable #5

## Quarterly horizon — Q2 2026 deliverables
1. **Genius excavation complete** — Genius Profile + Freedom Path shipped, voice samples captured — precondition for every downstream horizon
2. **First executor hire** — first DELEGATE-bucket role posted, interviewed, onboarded — serves annual ship #5
3. **First productized offer** — the Launch-Readiness Audit described, priced, sold to first 3 pilot clients — serves annual ship #4
4. **Book proposal drafted** — title, positioning, 3 sample chapter outlines — serves annual ship #3

## Ladder check
- Quarterly → Annual: **aligned** — every Q2 deliverable maps cleanly to an annual ship
- Annual → 3-year: **aligned** — 2026 ships directly produce 4 of 5 year-3 deliverables; year-3 deliverable #1 (book published) comes from year-2 work on the drafted proposal
- 3-year → 10-year: **aligned** — 20 trained practitioners at year 3 scales to 100+ at year 10; first book at year 3 is the foundation for the second and third at year 10
- 10-year → 30-year: **aligned** — a recognized practice-leading studio with 100+ trained practitioners is a direct contribution to the 30-year world where product operations is named as the design discipline it actually is

## Drift tests — ask yourself when a decision doesn't feel clean
1. Does this engagement let me do the diagnostic work only I can do, or am I about to become a ticket-routing project manager again?
2. Would the practice-leader I want to be in 10 years take this meeting? Or would she hand it off to a trained practitioner?
3. Is this a "yes" to an interesting opportunity that steals from the "yes" I already owe the book outline?
4. Am I pricing this like a consultant with imposter syndrome, or like the decision-architecture diagnostician the corpus proves I am?
5. Does the language in this proposal use my vocabulary — "decision debt", "the handoff", "friction budget", "load-bearing assumption" — or has it slipped back into corporate-consulting speak?
6. If a trained practitioner read this, would they recognize it as deploying my methodology, or as generic advice I could have written at any of my three past employers?
7. Does this decision serve the 3-year (book + 20 practitioners + stable consultancy) or am I drifting back into the operator-mode that kept me indispensable at past employers?

## Named next move
`/build-brand-kit maya-lindqvist` — the vision is named. Brand is next: the positioning, voice rules, palette, typography that compounds every piece of content Maya ships from here into the 30-year world she just named.
```
