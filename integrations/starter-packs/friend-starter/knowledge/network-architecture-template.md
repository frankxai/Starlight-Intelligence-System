## Filled example — "Maya, product-ops consultant" (fictional composite — every person in this map is invented; any resemblance to real persons is coincidental)

```
# Network Architecture — Maya Lindqvist — 2026-04-24

> Sovereignty is bilateral. Every person in this map is a full human, not a contact. This architecture is for your design, never for extraction.

## Inventory

**Total named:** 30 people
- Peers: 3
- Mentors: 5
- Mentees: 0
- Clients: 2
- Collaborators: 0
- Family: 0 (scoped out of work architecture by Maya's request)
- Acquaintances: 20

### Peers
- **Jonas E.** — ex-product-ops lead now building parallel practice in Rotterdam · Last: 2026-04-10, async · Freq: monthly · Trust: high · Flow: compounds
- **Wei L.** — former colleague at Company X, now VP Product at an IoT scale-up · Last: 2026-03, call · Freq: quarterly · Trust: medium · Flow: balanced
- **Sam K.** — developer Maya met at a hardware meetup, building ops tooling · Last: 2026-04-15, in-person · Freq: monthly · Trust: medium · Flow: compounds

### Mentors
- **Prof. Ingrid B.** — Maya's behavioral-economics thesis supervisor from her master's program · Last: 2026-03, call · Freq: quarterly · Trust: high · Flow: one-way gift inward
- **Tomas H.** — former COO at Company Y, advised Maya through a career inflection · Last: 2026-02, async · Freq: quarterly · Trust: high · Flow: one-way gift inward
- **Dr. Femi A.** — industrial-design studio lead from Maya's undergraduate years, her entry into cross-domain thinking · Last: 2025-12, dinner · Freq: yearly · Trust: high · Flow: one-way gift inward
- **Ruth O.** — independent consultant, 20 years ahead, Maya's business-of-consulting mentor · Last: 2026-03, call · Freq: monthly · Trust: high · Flow: compounds (Ruth learns from Maya's methodology; Maya learns from Ruth's business shape)
- **Karim D.** — Maya's longtime aikido teacher and informal counsel · Last: 2026-04-20, in-person · Freq: monthly · Trust: high · Flow: balanced

### Mentees
(none currently — gap)

### Clients
- **Company Zephyr (VP Product: Lena F.)** — 2024–present · Last: 2026-04-22, call · Freq: weekly during engagements, dormant between · Trust: high · Flow: compounds (Lena shares her launch insights back into Maya's methodology)
- **Company Kite (COO: Diego R.)** — 2025–present · Last: 2026-04-18, async · Freq: biweekly · Trust: medium · Flow: balanced

### Collaborators
(none currently — gap for joint artifact work)

### Acquaintances
- (20 named — former colleagues across 3 past employers, 3 meetup connections, 5 neighbors-in-industry Maya exchanges quarterly DMs with. Not detailed here for brevity. Status check: 3 are drifting toward "dormant"; 2 have moved into Maya's active work domain and should graduate to peer.)

## Strength audit — summary

- Compounds: 5 relationships — Jonas, Sam, Ruth, Company Zephyr, (gap: mentee)
- Balanced: 4 — Wei, Diego, Karim, one acquaintance
- One-way gifts inward: 3 mentors (Ingrid, Tomas, Femi) — Maya is under-thanking these people; reciprocity gap to close
- One-way gifts outward: 0 named (Maya's outward giving goes to clients inside paid engagements; no pro-bono gifts surfaced this cycle)
- Drains: 1 — an acquaintance from Company X who has been extractively asking for vendor introductions for 18 months without reciprocity. Named for visibility. Right-size.

## Top compounding leverage (3–5)

1. **Jonas × Maya methodology overlap** — Jonas is building a parallel product-ops practice in Rotterdam. Complementary geographies (Maya's is the Nordic hardware market), shared methodology. Highest-potential alliance candidate in the network.
2. **Sam's development capability × Maya's methodology** — Sam can build tooling that would turn Maya's "Decision Debt Ledger" and "Invisible Handoff Map" frameworks into a platform. Medium trust right now; needs trust-building + equity-clarity conversations. Medium-potential alliance candidate.
3. **Ruth's 20-year runway on Maya's business shape** — Ruth is the single highest-leverage relationship for Maya's consulting business decisions. Monthly cadence is right; protect it.
4. **Company Zephyr Lena → downstream introductions** — Lena has introduced Maya to 2 other product VPs in 2025. Highest introduction-graph node. Invest more.
5. **Prof. Ingrid B. → graduate program pipeline** — Ingrid is Maya's bridge to behavioral-economics graduate students who could become mentees and future collaborators. Underused leverage point. Ask Ingrid for one introduction this quarter.

## Gap archetypes

1. **No mentee** — Maya has been senior for 6 years and has taught 0 people her methodology outside paid client work. Compounding wisdom outward is missing; she is losing the teaching-is-learning loop. Search: 1–2 early-career product-ops people with behavioral-economics inclination, via Prof. Ingrid's graduate program pipeline.
2. **No collaborator for joint-artifact work** — Maya has rebuilt frameworks three times across three employers but never shipped a joint methodology artifact. The methodology whitepaper / book / course does not exist because she has no collaborator to compose with. Search: either Jonas (via alliance readiness) or a separate domain-expert writer.
3. **No protocol-defender voice in the network** — Maya's mentors are encouragers; her peers are peers; her clients are clients. Nobody in the network actively stress-tests Maya's decisions with authority. Search: one adversarial advisor, could be Ruth in a different mode or a new search.
4. **No peer in the consulting-business-shape stage** — Wei is at a scale-up; Jonas is building parallel practice; Sam is in tooling. Maya has no peer at her exact stage (solo consultant, methodology-carrying, 3-year runway). This is lonely and shows in business-decision velocity.

## Alliance candidates (surfaced only — not forged)

- **Jonas E.** — Pre-filter plausible on all 4: complementary geographies (Nordics vs Benelux), shared methodology (non-zero-sum artifact possible: joint methodology whitepaper + practice), sovereignty possible (geographies don't collide on decision rights), attestation likely wanted (both benefit from "Built on SIP" on joint methodology). **HIGH-potential.** Next: `/design-alliance-readiness Jonas`.
- **Sam K.** — Pre-filter plausible on 3 of 4: complementary skills (methodology × platform), non-zero-sum artifact (methodology-as-platform), sovereignty possible (Maya = methodology decisions, Sam = platform/tech decisions), attestation likely (Sam has signaled interest). Risk: trust is medium not high; equity split not yet clear. **MEDIUM-potential — develop first.** Next: `/design-alliance-readiness Sam` in one quarter after a trust-building joint project (small paid methodology-tooling engagement).

## Investment plan

### Invest more
- **Lena F. (Company Zephyr)** — highest introduction-graph node; monthly touch currently, but Maya could share more of the methodology work-in-progress with her
- **Ruth O.** — monthly is right; consider asking for the adversarial-advisor mode explicitly
- **Prof. Ingrid B.** — move from quarterly to monthly during the mentee-search quarter
- **Jonas E.** — weekly async touch during the pre-alliance assessment period

### Sustain
- Wei L., Tomas H., Dr. Femi A., Karim D., Diego R. — current rhythms are right

### Right-size
- **Draining acquaintance from Company X** — move from monthly DMs to quarterly, responses shorter, no new commitments. Warm, not severed.

### Graduate
- 2 acquaintances who have moved into active work domain — graduate to peer; specific invitation to a joint conversation, not just DM drift

### Complete
- 3 drifting acquaintances — warm closure: send one thoughtful message acknowledging the relationship's season, no forced continuation

## Cadence

### Weekly (1–3 named touches)
- Monday: Message Jonas about the joint-methodology outline — continue pre-alliance conversation
- Wednesday: Share the Company Zephyr handoff-map framework with Lena and ask what she'd add
- Friday: Async check-in with Ruth on the consulting-business question Maya is sitting on

### Monthly
- Mentor check-ins: Ingrid (mentee-pipeline question), Ruth (business-shape question), Karim (interior question). Tomas and Femi rotate quarterly.
- Draining-relationship triage: acquaintance from Company X — track how the right-sizing feels

### Quarterly
- Network review — confirm who compounded, who graduated, who right-sized. Re-run alliance-readiness on Jonas (3-month mark) and Sam (after trust-building joint project).

## Next move

Run `/design-alliance-readiness Jonas` this week. The four conditions look plausible on pre-filter, active trust is high, and Jonas has signaled interest in formalizing composition. This is the highest-leverage relational move in the architecture right now. Sam's readiness is a +1-quarter conversation after a trust-building joint project.
```

## Composition notes

This template composes with:

- **`/discover-genius`** — Genius Profile tells you *what* this person uniquely does; Network Architecture tells you *who* compounds with that
- **`/alliance-forge`** — Network Architecture surfaces candidates; `/design-alliance-readiness` filters them; only 4/4 candidates hand off to `/alliance-forge`
- **`/train-executor`** — an executor from the DELEGATE bucket may come from the network (acquaintance graduating to collaborator) or from outside; Network Architecture flags which
- **`/creator-pipeline`** — the creator pipeline's distribution layer compounds via introductions mapped here

## Sovereignty reminder

The Network Architecture lives in the person's instance only. Starlight does not retain relational graphs, names, or assessments for anyone. Every person named in the architecture is a full human with their own sovereignty. Nothing in this document is meant to manipulate, extract, or optimize against another person's interests. The map is for the person's own design — visibility into the relational layer they already inhabit.
