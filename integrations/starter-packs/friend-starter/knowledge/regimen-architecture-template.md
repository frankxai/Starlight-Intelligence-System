# Regimen Architecture — Template

> Structure for turning an energy audit + life context into a sustainable integrated regimen. Training + nutrition + sleep + stress + recovery as one system. Long-game over aesthetic-program. Not medical advice.

## Prerequisites

- Energy audit complete (`health/audit-<slug>-<date>.md`) OR retroactive audit with confidence flag.
- Life context understood: life stage, goals, available time, equipment, travel frequency, caregiving load.
- No active medical condition, injury in last 90 days, or clinician-supervised protocol that intersects with this regimen without clinician sign-off.

If any missing: halt and route back to `/energy-audit` or defer to clinician.

## Shape — ten sections

1. **Disclaimer** — non-medical advice statement; explicit deferral path
2. **Context** — life stage, goals, constraints, audit source, genius alignment
3. **Creative peaks to protect** — architectural anchors from audit or Genius Profile
4. **Weekly template** — 7-day table with training / nutrition / sleep / stress-output / protected peak
5. **Training program** — composed from `gym-training-expert`, 3-6 lines
6. **Nutrition strategy** — composed from `health-nutrition-expert`, 3-6 lines
7. **Sleep protocol** — bedtime / wake / hygiene / morning anchor
8. **Stress input/output balance** — honest inputs list, matched outputs list
9. **Cycles** — weekly rest, quarterly de-load, annual break, sprint protocol
10. **Weekly metrics + review cadence** — what to log, when to review
11. **Refused protocols** — named fads refused, with reason
12. **Next moves** — one named next command

## Example — filled at Ana grade

Ana is a composite example: mid-30s professional, sedentary history, transitioning from corporate burnout to sovereign practice. Uses this to show what a real first-pass regimen looks like. Not extreme. Sustainable. Designed to survive a bad week.

```
# Regimen Architecture — Ana — 2026-04-24 (v1.0)

## Disclaimer
This is not medical advice. It is evidence-based regimen architecture and self-audit framework. Consult a qualified clinician for diagnosis, treatment, injury, chronic condition, medication interaction, pregnancy, or any symptom that suggests a medical boundary. If any element of this regimen causes concerning symptoms, stop and consult your clinician.

## Context
- **Life stage:** sedentary-to-active transition. 8 years of office knowledge work, minimal structured training in last 3 years. No current injuries. No clinician-supervised protocols.
- **Goals:**
  - Sustain 3-4 hour creative peak windows 4 days/week (morning) for writing + client work.
  - Rebuild baseline strength over 6 months — a foundation, not a physique project.
  - Stop the Friday-afternoon emotional crash she logged 5 of 7 weeks.
  - Protect energy through the corporate-to-sovereign transition — this is a high-stress year.
- **Constraints:** ~45 min/day for training (not more). Home gym — dumbbells + pull-up bar + mat. Travels ~1 week/month. One child, school-age.
- **Audit source:** `health/audit-ana-2026-04-17.md`
- **Genius alignment:** Ana's Genius Profile shows creative peaks stacked Tue/Wed/Thu 08:30-11:30 — the regimen protects these.

## Creative peaks to protect
- **Tue / Wed / Thu 08:30-11:30** — stacked Mental + Creative peaks (4-5 on both dimensions). This is where writing + synthesis happens. Protected.
- **Sat 09:00-11:00** — secondary peak, less reliable; treat as upside.

## Weekly template

| Day | Training | Nutrition focus | Sleep | Stress-output | Protected peak |
|-----|----------|-----------------|-------|---------------|----------------|
| Mon | Strength A (45 min, 18:00) | Protein anchor ~120g (0.8g/lb × 150 lb), 3 meals, caffeine cutoff 13:00 | 22:30 / 06:30 | 20-min walk post-lunch | Deep work 09:00-12:00 (planning, not flagship creative) |
| Tue | Rest (or 30-min walk only) | Same. Breakfast 90 min before peak — oats + protein + berries | 22:30 / 06:30 | Outdoor coffee 07:00 (light anchor) | **08:30-11:30 PROTECTED PEAK — no meetings, phone on silent** |
| Wed | Strength B (45 min, 18:00) | Same | 22:30 / 06:30 | 20-min walk post-lunch | **08:30-11:30 PROTECTED PEAK** |
| Thu | Rest | Same | 22:30 / 06:30 | Outdoor coffee 07:00 | **08:30-11:30 PROTECTED PEAK** |
| Fri | Strength C (45 min, 12:00 — earlier to protect evening) | Same + slightly higher carbs (blood sugar stable through afternoon; addresses Fri-crash pattern) | 22:30 / 06:30 | **30-min walk 15:00 — preempt the Fri crash** | Light admin / client calls only |
| Sat | 45-min walk outdoors (family) | Flexible; protein anchor still hit | 23:30 / 07:30 (social drift ≤60 min) | Family + outdoor time = natural stress output | Optional creative block 09:00-11:00 |
| Sun | Full rest — no structured training | Same. 1 drink permitted with early dinner (not within 3h of sleep) | 22:30 / 06:30 | Sunday review 17:00 — regimen check-in | Rest and reset |

## Training program

From `gym-training-expert`: simple 3x/week full-body strength — Strength A (squat pattern + push + pull + core), Strength B (hinge pattern + push + pull + core), Strength C (squat pattern + push + pull + carry). 3 sets per compound, 2 sets per accessory. Progressive overload via rep-range (8-12) then weight. No hypertrophy-specialized split for first 6 months — foundation first. Reference: progressive overload + compound-first rationale per hypertrophy research consensus (Schoenfeld et al.).

## Nutrition strategy

From `health-nutrition-expert`: protein anchor ~120g/day (~0.8g/lb, 150 lb bodyweight) per hypertrophy research consensus for active adults. Three meals, no aggressive IF — her audit showed cognitive crash during long fasts. Carbs to ~200g/day (moderate, fiber-forward — vegetables, legumes, whole grains). Fats to balance. Hydration ~3L/day. Caffeine ~200mg/day, cutoff 13:00 (half-life 5-6h; sleep-research consensus). Alcohol ≤2 drinks/week, not within 3h of sleep (REM suppression).

## Sleep protocol
- Target hours: 7.5-8h
- Bedtime: 22:30 weeknights, 23:30 weekends max
- Wake time: 06:30 weekdays, 07:30 weekends (drift ≤60 min — social jet lag minimization, per circadian research)
- Pre-sleep rules: no screens after 21:30, no alcohol within 3h of bed, no heavy meals within 2h
- Morning anchor: 10-15 min outdoor light within first hour of waking, per Huberman/Walker research on morning sunlight + circadian anchoring.

## Stress input / output balance

**Inputs:**
- Corporate job, 40-45 hr/week (transition year — exit planned Q3)
- Parenting one school-age child
- Career transition uncertainty (high-grade chronic stress, identified in audit)
- Occasional client work building up sovereign practice

**Outputs:**
- Daily: 20-min walk post-lunch (4 weekdays) + morning outdoor light
- Weekly: 1 full rest day + Sunday 17:00 regimen check-in
- Monthly: one half-day outdoor block (hike, beach, park — named on calendar)
- Annual: ≥10-day break scheduled Q4

## Cycles

**Weekly:** Sunday is full rest — no structured training, no client work blocks.

**Quarterly de-load:** Last week of each quarter — training volume cut ~50% (2 short sessions instead of 3, lighter weights), sleep protected, social load reduced, nutrition maintained. Upcoming: week of 2026-06-22.

**Annual:** 10-day break scheduled for late November 2026. Maintain: sleep, protein anchor, daily walks. Loosen: training structure, macro precision. Do not attempt "vacation training" — counterproductive.

**Sprint protocol:** Ana's Q3 corporate-exit transition will be high-stress. Pre-exit week — training at ~60%, sleep protected non-negotiable, stress-outputs doubled (daily walks, one mid-week outdoor block). Post-exit week — de-load week.

## Weekly metrics to log
- Sleep hours (average)
- Training sessions (planned 3 / actual N)
- Energy rating 1-5: Physical / Mental / Emotional / Creative / Social
- Subjective recovery score 1-5 (Sunday evening)
- Early-warning flags: (1) training — 2 missed sessions no clear reason; (2) nutrition — 3 days under-eating or late caffeine; (3) sleep — 3 nights under 6h; (4) stress — sustained flat affect beyond 3 days; (5) recovery — skipping rest day because "I feel fine"

## Review cadence
- **Sunday 17:00 weekly** — 10-min self-check: what moved, what slipped, one fix for next week.
- **Last Sunday of each month** — 30-min regimen review: is it still fitting life, or has life changed?
- **Quarterly** — de-load week + regimen rewrite if life structure has shifted (e.g., post corporate exit Q3).

## Refused protocols (for record)
- **Keto-only** — refused; no medical indication, unsustainable for her cognitive demand pattern.
- **Under-4h sleep / hustle culture** — refused; her audit showed every predictable crash tied to a prior short-sleep night.
- **18:6 or stricter IF** — refused as default; her audit showed cognitive crashes in long-fast windows. Will revisit if goals change and audit justifies.
- **Chronic under-eating for body composition** — refused; sustainability gate fails. Body composition is a 12-18 month project at maintenance, not a cut.
- **Cleanses / detoxes / juice protocols** — refused; no research support for healthy adults.

2. `/define-vision` — align this substrate to Ana's 30-year arc (corporate-to-sovereign compound)

4. `health-nutrition-expert` — drill deeper into breakfast composition for protected-peak days
```
