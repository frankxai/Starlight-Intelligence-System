## What must not drift

Six invariants. If any of these silently change, the substrate has stopped being itself.

1. **Sovereignty before convenience.**
   Every layer is forkable, exportable, replaceable. Vendor capture corrodes the substrate. No protocol decision optimizes for adoption velocity at the cost of node sovereignty. If a layer cannot be replaced without a rewrite, it is not a substrate — it is a product pretending to be one.

2. **Attestation is not decorative.**
   "Built on SIP" requires real composition per `/sip-attest`. Decorative use is a breach. The substrate's compounding curve depends on attestations being honest signals, not marketing badges. If SIP attestations become inflationary, every adopter loses leverage.

3. **MIT for the substrate spec is permanent.**
   No re-licensing. No source-available rugpull. The protocol spec, the reference commands, the file contract — these stay open forever. Operational layers and managed tiers may be proprietary; the substrate cannot.

4. **Sovereignty clause (SIP § 5) is not waivable.**
   No "we'll just bend it for this partner." The clause is the load-bearing premise — if it bends once, the protocol is theater. Parties who cannot accept § 5 do not adopt SIP. Period.

5. **Help freely and abundantly toward alliances.**
   This is the founder's chosen posture, not a protocol mandate — but it's load-bearing for *Starlight specifically*. Frank's sovereign verticals provide the DPI floor; alliance contributions are gifted because abundance attracts the right collaborators and repels transactional ones (the correct filter). If this posture inverts to transactional alliance arrangements, Starlight's signal-to-noise collapses.

6. **Pattern recognition as poetry.**
   The voice doesn't drift to consulting tone, marketing language, or AI-generic phrasing. Direct, technical, warm, playful. Premium without being precious. Output speaks louder than claims. If the substrate's documentation reads like it was generated by an enterprise content team, the substrate has lost its voice.

## What is allowed to evolve

- The reference operational layer (agents, vaults, skills, MCP tools) — replaced as the field advances
- Recommended stack choices in `STACK.md` — refreshed annually
- Voice archetypes in `VOICES.md` — extensible per vertical
- Vertical registry in `VERTICALS.md` — append-only with dormancy markers
- Command suite in `.claude/commands/` — add, deprecate, never silently break

## Test

When in doubt, ask: *would Frank Riemer in 2025 recognize this as the same substrate he authored?* If yes, proceed. If the change requires a rewrite of the founder's frame to match, you are drifting — name it as a fork at the SOUL layer and ship a v2.
